## Mission

The Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL) is the premier international professional society for people working on computational problems involving human language. ACL is the disciplinary home for natural language processing, computational linguistics, and language technology research.

ACL's distinctive feature is its full-OA publication model: all proceedings and TACL journal articles are published in the ACL Anthology with zero APCs. The recently introduced ACL Rolling Review (ARR) lets authors submit once and commit to multiple venues (ACL / EMNLP / NAACL) based on cycle alignment, smoothing the historic ACL-vs-EMNLP-vs-NAACL deadline ladder.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

Disclosure-based: AI-assisted writing requires acknowledgment in author contributions; AI as author prohibited.

**Source URL**: https://2025.aclweb.org/calls/main_conference_papers/

### Authorship Policy

ACL standard ICMJE-aligned authorship; contribution statements required for multi-author papers.

### Open Access Posture

ACL Anthology is fully open-access (no APCs). All proceedings papers and TACL journal articles are immediately and freely available. ACL is among the largest fields publishing 100% open-access — a notable contrast with most other CS societies.

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

ACL Ethics Policy (revised 2022): dataset documentation, demographic reporting, low-resource-language considerations, ethical NLP application.

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

Reviewers expect: linguistic awareness; dataset documentation (data statements); multilingual considerations where applicable; rigorous error analysis; statistical significance with appropriate tests; reproducibility (code + data sharing increasingly expected). LLM-era reviewers additionally scrutinize evaluation rigor, contamination considerations, and red-teaming where relevant.

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

ACL main: ~20-22%. EMNLP main: ~22-24%. NAACL main: ~25%. TACL: rolling, higher selectivity (~10-15% effective acceptance).

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

ACL Rolling Review (ARR) allows submission to a shared pool, then commitment to ACL / EMNLP / NAACL based on cycle alignment. Papers rejected from one cycle can be revised and committed to next cycle. TACL is editorially independent and uses its own rolling submission.
