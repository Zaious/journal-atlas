## Mission

ACM SIGCHI is the premier international community for research in human-computer interaction. The society convenes scholars, practitioners, and designers studying the design, evaluation, and use of interactive technologies — from established areas (usability, interaction techniques, CSCW, ubiquitous computing) to emerging ones (AI-human interaction, accessibility, sustainable HCI, critical HCI).

SIGCHI sponsors or co-sponsors most of the field's flagship venues, including the CHI conference (the field's premier annual gathering) and the Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction (PACM HCI) journal series, which publishes papers presented at CSCW, IMWUT (UbiComp), and other SIGCHI-sponsored conferences.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

Disclosure-based: AI-assisted writing requires acknowledgment; AI listed as author prohibited.

**Source URL**: https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/new-acm-policy-on-authorship

### Authorship Policy

ACM authorship policy (revised 2023): contributions explicit; AI tools cannot be authors; authors responsible for AI-assisted content.

### Open Access Posture

ACM operates a hybrid model: subscription-default with Author-Paid OA via ACM Open Access (~$700-2500 per article). Many universities have ACM TAP (Transformative Agreement Program) agreements covering APC waivers. The Open TOC initiative makes ACM TOC indexes openly browsable.

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

COPE member; standard authorship/conflict-of-interest disclosure required; SIGCHI publication ethics committee handles disputes.

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

Across SIGCHI venues, reviewers expect: clear HCI contribution (empirical / artifact / methodological / theoretical); rigorous participant reporting (sample, consent, ethics); engagement with HCI canon; cross-discipline accessibility; mixed-methods literacy. Critical / value-sensitive design framings are increasingly welcomed across the community.

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

Most selective: CHI (~25-27% recent), UIST (~24-26%). Mid-tier: CSCW (~30-35%), DIS (~25%). Higher acceptance: IDC, IUI (~30-35%). PACM journals follow conference acceptance rates.

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

PACM CSCW and PACM IMWUT papers ARE CSCW and UbiComp conference papers (same submission pipeline). CHI rejection does not auto-transfer to other SIGCHI venues; each conference has its own annual cycle. Authors typically pivot manually to next-cycle CHI or sister conference.
