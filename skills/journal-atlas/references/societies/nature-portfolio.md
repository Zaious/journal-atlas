## Mission

Nature Portfolio publishes Nature (the world's most cited general science journal) and ~50 Nature-branded specialty and sister journals spanning biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, methods, communications, and broad-scope OA generalists. Across the portfolio, editorial culture emphasizes broad scientific significance combined with technical rigor.

The Nature Portfolio handles aggressive editorial triage — full-time professional editors (rather than academic volunteer editors) make decisive screening calls before peer review. Cross-discipline accessibility is a strong norm: even highly specialized work must read clearly to non-specialist scientists.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

Nature Portfolio AI Policy (2023): AI authorship prohibited; AI-assisted writing requires disclosure; large-language-model use must be described in methods.

**Source URL**: https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/ai

### Authorship Policy

ICMJE-aligned; CRediT contribution statements required across portfolio.

### Open Access Posture

Mixed: Nature flagship hybrid ($12,290 Gold OA). Nature Communications full OA (~$6,250 APC). Scientific Reports full OA (~$2,490, generalist). Read-and-Publish agreements widespread; many institutions cover Gold OA APCs.

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

Nature Portfolio publication ethics + COPE membership; strict data integrity, image manipulation detection (Image Integrity team), dual-use research scrutiny.

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

Nature Portfolio reviewers expect: broad-significance positioning, cross-discipline accessibility (cell biologist must follow computational paper, etc.), rigorous controls, comprehensive methodology. Nature flagship reviewers additionally evaluate field-changing impact. Specialty journals (Nature Methods / Biotech / etc.) emphasize community-adoption potential. Reviewers are typically asked: 'Is this significant for the broad readership?'

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

Nature flagship: ~8% (after >60% editorial desk-reject). Nature Communications: ~10-12% (after triage). Nature Methods: ~8-10% (manuscripts) + 75% desk-reject. Scientific Reports: ~40-50% (soundness-based generalist).

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

Nature flagship editors can recommend transfer to Nature Communications or specialty journals upon rejection; authors opt-in via a portal. Nature Portfolio has a robust internal cascade system smoothing the experience for authors.
