## Mission

Cell Press, an Elsevier division, publishes Cell and its sister specialty journals — collectively, the highest-impact biology journal portfolio in life sciences. Cell Press editorial culture emphasizes broad biological significance, mechanistic depth, and rigorous experimental validation across multiple model systems.

Compared to Nature Portfolio's similar broad-significance stance, Cell Press journals are typically more biology-deep (less cross-discipline) and place higher weight on complete mechanistic stories with comprehensive controls.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

Elsevier-aligned: disclosure required; AI as author prohibited; AI-assisted content authors' responsibility.

**Source URL**: https://www.cell.com/policies

### Authorship Policy

Cell Press requires CRediT contribution statements; co-corresponding author increasingly common.

### Open Access Posture

Cell flagship is hybrid (subscription default with $5,950 Gold OA option). Cell Reports is full OA. Cell Press supports green OA (preprints permitted on bioRxiv) and has transformative agreements with many institutions for OA APC waivers.

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

Elsevier publication ethics; COPE member; Cell journals strictly enforce data fabrication / image manipulation detection.

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

Cell Press reviewers expect: complete mechanistic story (not just phenotype); multiple experimental approaches converging on the same mechanism; rigorous controls; orthogonal validation; cross-species generalization where relevant; significance positioned for broad biology readership. Reviewer-required additional experiments are routine and substantial.

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

Cell flagship: ~8-12% (after >40% editorial triage). Specialty journals (Neuron, Immunity, etc.): ~15-20%. Cell Reports: ~20-25% (OA generalist, more accepting).

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

Cell editors can recommend transfer to Cell Reports or sister specialty journals on rejection; authors opt-in.
