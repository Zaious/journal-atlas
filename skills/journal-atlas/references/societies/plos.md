## Mission

PLOS is a pioneering non-profit Open Access publisher that helped establish full OA as a viable scholarly publishing model. PLOS journals span biology (PLOS Biology), computational biology (PLOS Computational Biology), medicine, ONE (generalist), and specialty venues. PLOS Biology, the flagship, is a Cell/Nature-tier OA alternative with rigorous selectivity; PLOS ONE pioneered the soundness-over-significance generalist model.

PLOS leads on OA advocacy, data-sharing mandates, and transparent peer review options. Many PLOS journals now offer published review reports as an author opt-in.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

PLOS AI Policy: disclosure required; AI as author prohibited; AI-generated content authors' responsibility.

**Source URL**: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ai-use

### Authorship Policy

ICMJE-aligned; CRediT contribution statements required.

### Open Access Posture

All PLOS journals are full Gold OA. APCs range from ~$1,805 (PLOS ONE) to ~$5,300 (PLOS Biology / Med). PLOS pioneers community-action pricing (CAP) for community ownership models and offers institutional agreements covering APCs. Data sharing is mandatory across the portfolio.

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

PLOS publication ethics; COPE member; strong data-availability requirements (mandatory across portfolio).

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

PLOS reviewers vary by journal: PLOS Biology (flagship) expects broad-biology significance + rigorous mechanism (similar to Cell / Nature). PLOS Computational Biology emphasizes methodological rigor + biological insight. PLOS ONE is soundness-based: technical correctness matters more than significance. All PLOS journals expect data sharing in compliant repositories.

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

PLOS Biology: ~15-20%. PLOS Computational Biology: ~25%. PLOS ONE: ~50%.

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

PLOS Biology editors can recommend transfer to PLOS sister journals (including PLOS ONE) upon rejection.
