## Mission

ACM SIGACCESS is the community for computer-accessibility research and practice. The society champions inclusive design, assistive technology, and participatory research with disabled communities. Its flagship conference (ASSETS) and journal (TACCESS) publish work spanning sensory, motor, cognitive, and intersectional accessibility, with strong norms around participatory methodology and disability-community framing.

SIGACCESS is the disciplinary home for researchers committed to disability justice and accessible-by-default design; reviewers expect work that centers disabled users as collaborators rather than research subjects.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

ACM-wide disclosure policy applies; SIGACCESS additionally emphasizes accessibility of AI-generated content (alt-text, plain-language summaries).

**Source URL**: https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/new-acm-policy-on-authorship

### Authorship Policy

ACM standard + SIGACCESS norm of including disabled co-authors where relevant ('Nothing about us without us').

### Open Access Posture

ACM hybrid OA. SIGACCESS actively encourages accessibility-of-publications (HTML versions, alt-text, accessible PDFs).

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

COPE member; SIGACCESS additionally emphasizes ethical participatory research with disabled communities, informed consent, accessibility of consent documents.

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

Reviewers expect: clear accessibility contribution; participatory methodology with disabled users (co-design preferred over study-of); demographic reporting includes disability; accessibility of submission documents (alt-text mandatory); engagement with disability-studies and critical-access literature.

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

ASSETS: ~25-30% (2023-2024). TACCESS: longer-form revisions; ~30-40% after R&R rounds.

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

ASSETS papers can revise + resubmit to TACCESS in longer format. Cross-pollination with SIGCHI (CHI accessibility track) is common.
