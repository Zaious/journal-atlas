## Mission

The American Psychological Association is the largest scientific and professional organization representing psychology in the United States. APA publishes the most influential journals across nearly every psychology subfield (experimental, social, personality, clinical, developmental, educational, industrial-organizational, etc.) and sets the field's dominant style guide (APA Style, now in 7th edition).

APA editorial culture is methodologically rigorous, statistically demanding, and increasingly committed to open science. Many APA journals now require pre-registration for confirmatory work, code and data sharing, and demographic reporting.

## Society-wide Policies

### AI Use Policy

APA AI Policy (2023): disclosure required; AI as author prohibited; authors responsible for AI-generated content correctness.

**Source URL**: https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/resources/policy-publishing-ai-assisted

### Authorship Policy

APA-style strict ICMJE-aligned authorship; CRediT contribution statement required.

### Open Access Posture

APA journals are subscription-default with Author-Paid OA option (~$3,000-3,500 APC). Many journals support transformative agreements via APA + university libraries. APA PsycNet provides centralized access for institutional subscribers.

### Conflict of Interest / Publication Ethics

APA Ethics Code governs research and publication; IRB strictly required for human-subjects; data fabrication / plagiarism handled by APA Publications Ethics Office.

## Editorial Culture

### Common Reviewer Expectations Across Society Venues

APA reviewers expect: pre-registration for confirmatory work (increasingly mandatory), appropriate statistical power, effect sizes with confidence intervals, demographic reporting, code + data sharing (or rationale for not sharing), engagement with relevant APA-style citation conventions. Reviewer pool is highly methodological; statistical rigor matters across subfields.

### Acceptance Rate Patterns

Flagship journals (JPSP, JEP family): ~10-15%. Specialty journals (J Counseling Psych, Psych & Aging, JEAP): ~15-25%. APA Open Access journals (newer): higher acceptance.

### Inter-venue Cascade / Transfer

No formal cascade. APA journals are editorially independent. Authors typically pivot manually to APA sister journal or non-APA peer.
