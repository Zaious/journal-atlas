### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** No theoretical school is mandated: the guidelines say the journal publishes fundamental research about organizations and their processes, structures, technologies, identities, forms and people, welcomes diverse methods with best practices for each, and particularly appreciates papers that connect theoretical perspectives (paraphrase; https://web.archive.org/web/20260308104242/https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/orsc/submission-guidelines ).
- **What kind of engagement?** Research grounded in fields such as organizational behaviour and theory, psychology, sociology, economics, strategy, information systems, innovation, entrepreneurship or cognitive science, at levels from individuals to organizations (same page). Perspectives must make a substantial contribution to theory of broad interest, as logical argument and not editorial opinion (https://pubsonline.informs.org/pb-assets/filesorsc/PerspectivesGuidanceFinal-1722256444370.pdf).
- **Article type and length**: original research under about 50 double-spaced pages all-inclusive, abstract at most 250 words, 3 to 10 keywords (guidelines; length policy https://pubsonline.informs.org/pb-assets/filesorsc/PageLengthPolicyFinal-1728561990830.pdf).
- **Special-issue framing seen**: a migration-and-organizations call with a 2021-12-31 deadline on the 2023-12-09 copy of the calls page (https://web.archive.org/web/20231209190508/https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/orsc/calls-for-papers ); no current call was read.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: the Editor-in-Chief reports that desk rejections exceeded 50% of submissions in 2025 and that nearly all primarily AI-written papers were desk rejected (https://orgsci.substack.com/p/the-annual-report); an excessively long paper may be desk rejected with an option to resubmit shorter (length policy PDF). The journal publishes no desk-rejection reasons beyond these statements.

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Double-blind preparation**: names, institutions and acknowledgments must be removed and self-references neutral (Peer Review; guidelines).
- **Transparency checklist**: the submitting author must certify compliance with the Data and Methods Transparency Policy by upload; papers using illegally obtained data need the Editor-in-Chief's prior written approval (Practical Concerns; checklist PDF).
- **No simultaneous review**: a manuscript cannot be under review elsewhere; conference submission at the same time is allowed (Preprint Policy; guidelines).
- **Authors must nominate no conflicted reviewers or editors**: the form asks for explicit confirmations (Practical Concerns).
- No other hard blocker is supported by a fact I could read.

### Soft Tax

- **Selectivity**: desk rejections exceeded 50% in 2025 and submissions rose 25% (Metrics; Editor-in-Chief's 2026-02-21 report). These are editor-reported figures.
- **Length**: under about 50 double-spaced pages all-inclusive, or the editor may desk reject with an option to resubmit shorter (Format).
- **Time to print**: about 9 months from acceptance to an issue as of the 2025-02-05 report, though papers appear online within days (Review Cycle Time).
- **Reciprocity and process steps**: ORCID iD for the submitting author, a transparency checklist upload, conflict confirmations (Format; Practical Concerns).
- **Open access cost**: 3,000 US dollars on a 2022 copy of the Open Option page, amount unverified now (Open Access).

### Best Suited For

- Empirical work on organizations, quantitative or qualitative: quantitative terms in 28.1% and interview or qualitative terms in 7.8 to 8.4% of abstracts (Methodological Preferences).
- Work on technology, AI and innovation, and on institutions, power, stakeholders and identity: the most-cited records and the largest framework counts (Epistemological & Political Leanings; Sensitive Topics).
- Papers that connect theoretical perspectives or study understudied settings: both are named as appreciated in the guidelines (Framing Requirements).
- Authors who want fast editor decisions: the Editor-in-Chief reports desk rejects averaging 11 days and first-round reviews in the low-to-mid 70s of days (Review Cycle Time; self-reported).

### Not Recommended For

- Purely conceptual papers unless they fit the Perspectives route: Perspectives are occasional (Format), and "conceptual" appears in 6.3% of abstracts (Methodological Preferences).
- Primarily AI-written manuscripts: the Editor-in-Chief reports that nearly all such papers were desk rejected in 2025 (Framing Requirements).
- Authors needing a verified acceptance rate or AI policy text: neither could be read (Metrics; AI Policy).
- Decolonial, Indigenous or religion-centred work as the main contribution: 0.3 to 0.5% of abstracts match (Sensitive Topics); this shows low frequency, not a stated policy.
