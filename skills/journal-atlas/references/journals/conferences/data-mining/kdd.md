## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Toy-scale empirical claims (especially for Research track)
- ADS track without real deployment narrative
- Missing baselines from recent state-of-the-art

### Soft Tax

Medium-High — scale of experiments required is substantial

### Best Suited For

- Large-scale data mining algorithms
- Web-scale systems with empirical validation
- ADS track: real-deployment lessons-learned papers
- Social network analysis / graph algorithms

### Not Recommended For

- Toy-scale demonstrations without scaling argument
- Pure theory without empirical validation
