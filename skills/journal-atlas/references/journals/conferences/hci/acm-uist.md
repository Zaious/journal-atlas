## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Generic 'we built a tool' submissions without user study or design contribution
- Pure cognitive psychology or social psychology submissions without HCI artifact / interaction angle
- Quantitative-only claims from small-N studies without methodological justification
- Missing participant reporting (demographics, consent, IRB, ethical considerations)

### Soft Tax

Variable. Empirical HCI: low. Third-wave / critical / autoethnographic: moderate (requires explicit HCI canon engagement). Non-HCI-originating manuscripts: high (substantial reframing through HCI lens).

### Best Suited For

- Empirical HCI papers (user studies, mixed-methods field deployments)
- Design research with artifact + evaluation
- HCI methodology / theory contributions
- CSCW, accessibility, value-sensitive design
- AI / ML interaction, mixed-reality, mobile and ubiquitous computing

### Not Recommended For

- Pure cognitive/social psychology without HCI artifact
- Pure design studies without empirical evaluation
- Manuscripts targeting non-HCI audiences primarily
