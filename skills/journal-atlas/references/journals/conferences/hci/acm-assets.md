## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Pure technical work without target-population (disability / affect) evaluation
- Missing participant reporting (consent, ethical review, demographics)
- Inaccessible submission documents (no alt-text, no accessible PDF)

### Soft Tax

Variable. Pure technical work: moderate (requires accessibility / affect framing). Critical / participatory work: low (already aligned).

### Best Suited For

- Accessibility research with disabled participants (participatory / co-design)
- Assistive technology design and evaluation
- Inclusive / universal design contributions
- Cross-disability and intersectional accessibility work

### Not Recommended For

- General HCI work that incidentally addresses accessibility
- Pure technical assistive-tech work without user-population evaluation
- Accessibility-policy work without empirical / design contribution
