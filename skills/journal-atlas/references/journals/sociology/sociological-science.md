## Strategic Notes

> Each statement follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where evidence is missing the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **No revise-and-resubmit**: after the first assessment the paper is accepted, conditionally accepted or rejected (Review Cycle Time; Peer Review).
- **Replication package** for statistical or computational work, as a condition of publication (Practical Concerns).
- **Originality**: no prior publication in whole or part in another peer-reviewed outlet; iThenticate check (Practical Concerns).
- **Fees**: a $45 submission fee and a publication fee at acceptance, unless waived (Open Access).
- **AI disclosure**: an AI disclosure statement at the end of the manuscript, covering text, code, data analysis and literature-search uses (AI Policy).

### Soft Tax

- **Cost**: publication fee grows with length and rank; for a full professor it starts at $450 plus $20 per 500 words (Open Access).
- **Production**: accepted papers are typeset in LaTeX; authors supply LaTeX or pay $50 to $200 for conversion (Format).
- **Concision**: long literature reviews lower the odds of acceptance (Framing Requirements).
- **Selectivity**: 20% accepted of 207 manuscripts, editor-supplied to SciRev, year unstated (Metrics).

### Best Suited For

- Concise, rigorous sociological papers in any subfield that can be judged as written, with a decision wanted within about 30 days (Review Cycle Time; Framing Requirements).
- Quantitative, experimental or computational work with shareable code and data (Format; Methodological Preferences).
- Replications, falsification studies and comments on published work, which the journal invites (Format).

### Not Recommended For

- Authors who want reviewer-guided revision rounds: the journal is evaluative, not developmental (Framing Requirements).
- Authors who cannot pay fees and do not qualify for a waiver (Open Access).
- Work needing a stated preprint policy before submission: none was found (Preprint Policy).
