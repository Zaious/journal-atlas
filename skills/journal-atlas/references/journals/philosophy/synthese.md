## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Survey articles, response-only papers and brief discussion notes are not considered, and a rejected manuscript cannot be resubmitted even in heavily revised form (Format > Article types accepted; Peer Review > Reviewer recommendations).
- The manuscript must be fully anonymised, with self-citation in the third person during review (Peer Review > Type; Voice & Style).
- The paper must engage in detail with current philosophical literature and show an advance over it (Framing Requirements > Mandatory framing).
- Offensive, discriminatory or impolite-toned writing is excluded outright (Sensitive Topics).

### Soft Tax

- Length: no hard limit, but 15 to 30 printed pages is the stated rule of thumb (Format > Word limit).
- Time: publisher dates for accepted papers show a median of 245 days from received to accepted (n=2,779), SciRev shows a first review round of 4.0 months and total handling of 5.5 months (n=6), and one reported desk rejection took about three months despite a stated four-day median (Review Cycle Time).
- A "Statements and Declarations" section is mandatory, and LLM use must be documented in the Methods (Format; AI Policy).
- Open access, if wanted or required by a funder, costs £2,290, $3,190 or €2,590 (Open Access > APC).
- De-anonymisation step after "accept but incomplete" before final acceptance (Peer Review > Typical R+R rounds).

### Best Suited For

- Epistemology, metaphysics, logic and philosophy-of-science papers with a stated advance over the literature: epistem wording appears in 27.8% of records, metaphysics-related in 22.2%, logic or mathematics in 15.2% (Orientation; Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Formal-methods and model-based philosophy: a named area, and simulation, modelling or computational wording appears in 6.7% of records (Framing Requirements > Topical areas; Methodological Preferences).
- Philosophy of machine learning and data science, and social epistemology of injustice and marginalisation: three of the five most-cited records of the window concern machine learning or statistics and two concern epistemic injustice (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Topics matching an open collection, such as language, power and social justice (Framing Requirements > Open calls).

### Not Recommended For

- Short notes, replies or survey articles (Hard Blockers).
- Authors who want a published turnaround guarantee or a second submission of a revised rejected paper (Review Cycle Time; Hard Blockers).
- Papers whose main contribution is empirical data with little engagement with the philosophical literature: the journal's own criterion is engagement with philosophical literature, and "experiment" and "survey" wording appear in 5.4% and 1.2% of records (Framing Requirements; Methodological Preferences).
- Work on climate or COVID-19 as such: 0.5% and 0.3% of records mention them in this window (Sensitive Topics).
