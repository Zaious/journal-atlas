## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate based on family norm; submission experience welcome to refine)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope statement.

### Best Suited For

- Novel bioinformatics methods/algorithms with biological validation
- Tool papers with broad community-adoption potential
- Computational genomics / transcriptomics / proteomics
- ML/AI applications to biological data with reproducible benchmarks

### Not Recommended For

- Pure biology papers without computational methods contribution
- Tool papers without comparison against existing alternatives
- Single-dataset analyses without methodological novelty
