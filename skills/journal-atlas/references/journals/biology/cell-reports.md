- **Mandatory framing?** Yes — clear biological mechanism or community-adoptable methods contribution required

## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate based on family norm; submission experience welcome to refine)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope statement.

### Best Suited For

- Broad-scope cell/molecular biology with strong reproducibility
- Papers rejected from Cell that retain significance / mechanism
- Mechanistic studies with cross-discipline appeal
- OA-mandate authors needing Cell Press venue

### Not Recommended For

- Highly specialized subfield work (consider Cell Press specialty journals)
- Incremental confirmatory studies
- Pure methods papers (consider Cell Reports Methods)
