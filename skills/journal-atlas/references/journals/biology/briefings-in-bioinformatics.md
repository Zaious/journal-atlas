## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Review articles on bioinformatics methods / software / databases
- Comparative software reviews with rigorous benchmarking
- Cross-method comparison studies
- Educational surveys for the bioinformatics community

### Not Recommended For

- Original research papers (consider Bioinformatics or NAR instead)
- Single-tool descriptions without comparative context
- Pure biology reviews without computational angle
