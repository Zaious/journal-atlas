## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Genomics research with mechanistic or evolutionary insight
- Functional genomics / epigenomics studies
- Method papers for genomic analysis (with biological validation)
- Population / comparative genomics

### Not Recommended For

- Pure database papers (consider NAR Database Issue)
- Single-organism phenotypic studies without genomic depth
- Pure clinical genomics without methodological / mechanistic contribution
