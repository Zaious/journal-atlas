## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Original biology research with strong reproducibility commitments
- Authors comfortable with the public 'Reviewed Preprints' model (post-2023)
- Cross-discipline biology work
- Authors valuing transparent peer review (reviews published with paper)

### Not Recommended For

- Authors needing JCR Impact Factor for tenure/career evaluation
- Confidential / sensitive work (review reports are public)
- Authors avoiding preprint-first models
