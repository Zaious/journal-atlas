**Method notes:** Crossref abstracts were stripped of markup and counted locally with the regexes below (Python re, IGNORECASE except the first-person pattern). Counts are over records that have an abstract (title plus abstract text); share = hits / 119. Regex hits count records, not occurrences. ## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Data reporting**: manuscripts without information on how the data were collected, or with unsourced secondary examples, are returned before review (Practical Concerns).
- **Anonymisation**: double-blind; separate title page, no identifying self-citation (Peer Review).
- **Glossing**: Leipzig glossing conventions are required for glossed examples, with additions noted in a footnote (Format).
- **Scope**: the journal publishes theoretical research on grammatical data; a paper without grammatical data or theory falls outside the aims (Framing Requirements).
- **Prior publication**: submission implies the work is unpublished and not under consideration elsewhere (Practical Concerns).

### Soft Tax

- **Length**: up to 40 single-spaced pages for full articles and 15 for short ones; longer manuscripts are "unlikely to be reviewed expeditiously" (Format).
- **Cost**: hybrid; the open-access route costs GBP 2290, USD 3190 or EUR 2590 unless an institutional agreement applies (Open Access).
- **Timing is uncertain**: the page prints an 11-day median to first decision, while SciRev n = 5 reports first rounds of 10 to 26 weeks and 20 to 35 weeks of total handling for accepted papers (Review Cycle Time).
- **Documentation work**: consultant details, data sources and page numbers for secondary examples must be written out (Practical Concerns).

### Best Suited For

- Theoretical syntax, morphology, semantics and phonology that rests on detailed data from a named language: Syntax 55.5%, Morphology 34.5%, Semantics 32.8%, Phonology 21.0% of abstract-records, and the most-cited records treat Washo, Latin, Russian, Igbo and Turkish (Orientation, Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Work on under-described languages that reports its data sources carefully (Practical Concerns, Methodological Preferences).
- Short empirical-and-theoretical notes: the short-article category allows up to 15 pages (Format).

### Not Recommended For

- Corpus-statistical, computational, acquisition or sociolinguistic studies: each appears in under 3.5% of abstract-records (Methodological Preferences, Orientation).
- Applied or language-in-society work, which the corpus covers elsewhere (see the Applied Linguistics entry); no count here supports it.
- Authors who need a published acceptance rate or firm timing before choosing: none is stated, and the timing sources disagree (Metrics, Review Cycle Time).
