## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Sound original research that lacks broad-Nature-significance angle
- OA-mandate authors needing Nature Portfolio venue without selectivity barriers
- Cross-discipline work that doesn't fit specialty journals
- Authors rejected from Nature / Nature Communications on novelty grounds

### Not Recommended For

- High-impact discoveries (consider Nature / Nature Comms)
- Specialty subfield work better served by domain-specific journal
- Authors needing high-IF venue for career evaluation (3.8 IF)
