### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** The scope is research that makes "significant contributions to conversations about global politics, broadly defined", attentive to historical and contemporary dynamics (about page, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/review-of-international-studies/information/about-this-journal; retrieved 2026-11-23). No topic or theory is mandated: contributions are judged "regardless of theoretical orientation or empirical focus" (why-choose page).
- **What kind of engagement?** The page welcomes theoretically informed, empirically rich and methodologically rigorous articles, including interdisciplinary research (about page and preparing-your-materials page).
- **Article type and length**: research articles of 8,500 to 12,000 words including notes and references (see Format).
- **Special-issue framing seen**: special issues follow an annual formal call; among the most-cited records is a special-issue introduction on pluriversal relationality (https://doi.org/10.1017/s0260210522000389).
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: submissions outside the word range or with an inaccurate word count may be desk rejected (preparing-your-materials page, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/review-of-international-studies/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials). No other reason for rejection is stated.
