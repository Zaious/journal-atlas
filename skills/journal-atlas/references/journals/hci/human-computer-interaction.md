### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes (soft)
- **If yes, what kind?** Manuscripts must engage **HCI theory or methodology** with substantive empirical or conceptual contribution. The journal's editorial tradition values theoretical depth alongside empirical rigor.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: Reviewers will probe contribution clarity; pure design reports or pure psychology may be redirected.

## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Manuscripts without clear HCI contribution
- Submissions framed primarily for cognitive-psychology or pure-design audiences
- Failure to disclose AI use per T&F publisher policy
- Pure system descriptions without empirical or conceptual evaluation

### Soft Tax

Low for classical-HCI empirical papers. Moderate for design-research or critical-HCI submissions — requires foregrounding the HCI theoretical contribution. T&F editorial cycle typically slower than ACM TOCHI.

### Best Suited For

- Classical HCI empirical and theoretical papers
- Cognitive-engineering, distributed-cognition, mental-model studies
- Mixed-methods HCI evaluations
- Methodological contributions to HCI research
- Theoretical syntheses bridging HCI subfields

### Not Recommended For

- Pure design provocations (consider ACM Interactions)
- Pure cognitive psychology without HCI artifact
- Authors needing rapid turnaround (T&F cycle is moderate)
- Submissions requiring open-access immediately (T&F hybrid subscription default)

### Rejection Fallback Chain

| Rank | Journal | Why this fallback works |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | [International Journal of Human-Computer Studies](./international-journal-of-human-computer-studies.md) | Closest peer in scope; Elsevier (vs T&F); cognitive-leaning HCI |
| 2 | [ACM TOCHI](./acm-transactions-on-computer-human-interaction.md) | If the paper has stronger interaction-design or system contribution |
| 3 | [International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction](./international-journal-of-human-computer-interaction.md) | Broader / design-leaning T&F HCI; somewhat more accommodating |

**Reverse direction** *(optional)*: If your paper was rejected from TOCHI as "more theoretical than systems-focused," HCI journal (T&F) is often a better fit because: it values theoretical contributions equally with empirical work; classical-HCI canonical engagement is welcomed; and its editorial culture rewards theoretical depth over artifact novelty.
