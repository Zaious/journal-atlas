## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Historical design research with archival rigor
- Cross-cultural design history
- Material-culture and design studies
- Design education historiography

### Not Recommended For

- Contemporary design research (consider Design Studies / Design Issues)
- HCI-engineering work with no historical framing
- Pure art history without design focus
