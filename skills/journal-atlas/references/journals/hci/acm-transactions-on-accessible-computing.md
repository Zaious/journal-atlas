## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate based on family norm; submission experience welcome to refine)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope statement.

### Best Suited For

- Accessibility research with disabled users (participatory / co-design)
- Assistive technology design and evaluation
- Inclusive / universal design contributions
- Long-form accessibility research (vs. ASSETS conference 4-8pp)

### Not Recommended For

- Accessibility-adjacent work without primary accessibility contribution
- Pure technical work without user-population evaluation
- Mainstream HCI work that incidentally mentions accessibility
