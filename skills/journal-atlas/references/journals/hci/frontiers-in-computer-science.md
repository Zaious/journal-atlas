## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Inability to fit any Frontiers article type
- APC inability to pay (no general waiver fund — institution / country-based)
- Failure to disclose AI use per Frontiers policy
- Manuscripts outside the journal's CS / HMI / AI scope

### Soft Tax

Variable. For HCI-conceptual papers fitting Hypothesis & Theory: low-moderate. For longer empirical work: moderate — must articulate article-type fit clearly. APC ($1,150) lower than Frontiers in Psychology — more accessible for independent / lower-budget researchers. Currency risk (CHF-invoiced) noted but moderate compared to higher-APC journals.

### Best Suited For

- HMI section: HCI papers with empirical or conceptual contribution
- AI ethics / AI in services research
- VR / AR user experience studies
- Hypothesis & Theory papers proposing new HCI / CS conceptual frameworks
- Perspective and Opinion pieces on CS / HMI trends
- Authors needing rapid OA visibility with moderate APC

### Not Recommended For

- Authors needing top-tier HCI reputation signaling exclusively (consider TOCHI, HCI journal)
- Pure design provocations (consider ACM Interactions)
- Authors prioritizing zero-APC paths (consider subscription HCI journals)
- Methodologically-rigorous studies needing established-journal-prestige signaling

### Rejection Fallback Chain

| Rank | Journal | Why this fallback works |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | [AI & Society](./ai-and-society.md) | If the piece engages AI ethics / society; Springer hybrid; broader audience |
| 2 | [ACM Interactions](./acm-interactions.md) | If the piece is provocation / manifesto style |
| 3 | [International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction](./international-journal-of-human-computer-interaction.md) | If the piece is applied HCI with empirical evaluation |

**Reverse direction** *(optional)*: If your paper was rejected from a more selective HCI venue, Frontiers in Computer Science HMI section is often a better fit because: it offers OA visibility with moderate APC; Hypothesis & Theory and Perspective article types open routes for non-traditional contributions; and the section-specific editorial structure provides focused review.
