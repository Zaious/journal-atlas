## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate based on family norm; submission experience welcome to refine)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope statement.

### Best Suited For

- CSCW / social computing research presented at the CSCW conference
- Empirical studies of online communities, work collaboration, social platforms
- Mixed-methods HCI research with rich qualitative components
- Critical / value-sensitive design work on collaborative systems

### Not Recommended For

- Pure interaction-technique demonstrations (consider UIST / DIS)
- Cognitive psychology without CSCW angle
- AI/ML systems papers without collaboration / human-team focus
