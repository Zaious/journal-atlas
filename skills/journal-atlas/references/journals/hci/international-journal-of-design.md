### Best Suited For

- OA design-research papers (no APC barrier)
- Design methodology contributions
- Cross-cultural / non-Western design work
- Authors valuing Diamond OA with no fees

### Not Recommended For

- Hard HCI-engineering work (consider CHI / IJHCS)
- Pure design-practice papers without research methodology
- Industry-magazine-style design articles (consider Interactions magazine)
