### Best Suited For

- Industry-practitioner-oriented pervasive computing work
- Bridge papers connecting research to practice
- Authors preferring shorter / more accessible format

### Not Recommended For

- Deep technical research with extensive methodology (consider PACM IMWUT)
- Pure theoretical / formal-methods work
- Highly specialized subfield work needing peer-research depth
