### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes (soft)
- **If yes, what kind?** Manuscripts must be framed as **review, integrative, theoretical, or historical** contributions — not original empirical studies. Even empirical-flavored work must position itself as a synthesis or critical review.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: Desk reject likely if the manuscript reads as a primary empirical report; redirect to other APA outlets (e.g., *Psychological Bulletin* for meta-analyses, division-specific journals for empirical work).

### Reviewer Pool Characteristics

Reviewers are drawn primarily from the APA Division 1 (Society for General Psychology) network and broader integrative-psychology community. Expect generalist breadth rather than specialist depth — reviewers value clear positioning across psychology subfields and historical/conceptual rigor. Common reviewer demands: "How does this contribute to general psychology rather than [narrow subfield]?" and "Where is the integrative synthesis across traditions?"

## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Original empirical studies (redirect to subfield journals)
- Manuscripts framed as personal essay without integrative or historical synthesis
- Submissions over 15,000 words (returned for trimming)
- AI-generated content without disclosure (per APA / Sage guidelines)

### Soft Tax

Low for well-framed integrative work. Authors typically invest ~1 revision session to: (1) broaden the disciplinary positioning beyond a single subfield, (2) strengthen historical / theoretical scaffolding. Citation tax: reviewers expect engagement with the journal's own integrative-review tradition and APA Division 1 historical work.

### Best Suited For

- Integrative theoretical reviews bridging psychology subfields
- Historical analyses of psychological constructs or research programs
- Critical conceptual papers proposing new general frameworks
- Meta-analyses with strong theoretical framing
- Global / indigenous perspectives on general psychology

### Not Recommended For

- Original empirical reports (quantitative or qualitative)
- Narrow-subfield methodological contributions
- Pure autoethnography without integrative-review framing
- Replication studies or registered reports
- Clinical-application papers

### Rejection Fallback Chain

| Rank | Journal | Why this fallback works |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | [Theory & Psychology](./theory-and-psychology.md) | Similar theoretical-conceptual scope; more flexible on critical / continental framings; smaller word limit (8K standard, 10K extensible) but no theoretical-purity ceiling |
| 2 | [New Ideas in Psychology](./new-ideas-in-psychology.md) | Welcomes "speculative, fundamental, theoretical, and integrative" work; broader stylistic acceptance; comparable Elsevier reputation |
| 3 | [Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences](./phenomenology-and-the-cognitive-sciences.md) | Stronger fit if integrative argument has phenomenological or 4E-cognition grounding; no hard word limit |

**Reverse direction** *(optional)*: If your paper was rejected from a more selective generalist outlet (e.g., *Psychological Bulletin*, *Perspectives on Psychological Science*), RGP is often a better fit because: it accepts broader theoretical synthesis without requiring effect-size meta-analysis machinery; its scope explicitly includes "historical, theoretical, or critical studies"; and word-limit flexibility (up to 15K) accommodates longer integrative arguments.
