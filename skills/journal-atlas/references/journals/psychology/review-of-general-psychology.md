Accepted: theoretical, conceptual and methodological articles of normally 5,000 to 10,000 words.
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** return or desk rejection, as stated above. The earlier claim that a primary empirical report would be redirected to other APA outlets such as *Psychological Bulletin* has no source and is dropped. ## Strategic Notes

Each line names the recorded fact it rests on.

### Hard Blockers

- Manuscripts outside the stated scope: the guide says an article "must be within the scope of the journal and be of sufficient quality. If not, it will not be reviewed" (Format > Article types accepted; Framing Requirements).
- A manuscript that is not fully anonymized or lacks the Statements and Declarations: such manuscripts are returned for amendment before peer review (Policies > Peer Review; Format > Other requirements).
- Above 15,000 words including references: the guide's stated ceiling (Format > Word limit).
- Undisclosed generative AI use that affects methods, analysis, results or conclusions: the editor may reject at any stage (Policies > AI Policy).
- Updating a preprint on a server during peer review: against the guide (Policies > Preprint Policy).

### Soft Tax

Length: normally 5,000 to 10,000 words, extendable to 15,000 depending on topic, references included (Format > Word limit). Formatting: unstructured abstract of at most 200 words, 3 to 7 keywords, anonymized manuscript plus a separate Title Page with declarations and CRediT roles (Format). Framing: the stated scope is theoretical, conceptual and methodological; an empirical manuscript should argue its conceptual or methodological contribution in the cover letter, which the guide describes as helping the Editor's preliminary evaluation (Framing Requirements; this is my inference, no page states a rejection pattern). Reference-style uncertainty: the archived guide does not show which style applies, so check the live guide before formatting (Format > Reference limit).

### Best Suited For

- Integrative or conceptual reviews that cut across subdisciplines: the home-page scope plus theoretical/conceptual in 60 of 168 abstracts and integrat* in 36 (Orientation).
- Historical analyses of psychology: historical vocabulary in 40 of 168 abstracts (Orientation).
- Decolonial, anti-racist and global-perspective work: such vocabulary in 17 and 20 of 168 abstracts, and 6 of the 12 most-cited 2021-2026 works (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Longer arguments: the ceiling is 15,000 words including references (Format).
- Preprint-first authors: preprints accepted and the accepted manuscript may be shared at any time under Sage's archiving page (Policies > Preprint Policy).

### Not Recommended For

- Manuscripts that would need more than 15,000 words including references (Format).
- Primary empirical reports with no conceptual or methodological claim: scope sentence plus experiment vocabulary in only 10 of 168 abstracts, many in non-empirical pieces (Framing Requirements; Methodological Preferences).
- Authors who need a quick decision time: no turnaround figure was found on any page read (Metrics > Review Cycle Time).
- Topic-fit check for sex work, suicide or drug-use work: 0 of 173 records each, and kink and extremism appear only as passing mentions (Sensitive Topics). This does not show such work would be refused.
