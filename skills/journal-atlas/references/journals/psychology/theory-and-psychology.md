### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Stated as scope, not as a submission rule. The journal describes itself as a forum for "theoretical and meta-theoretical analysis in psychology" and says its aim is to foster "theoretical dialogue and innovation" (home page snapshot 2025-06-26: https://web.archive.org/web/20250626194225/https://journals.sagepub.com/home/tap ).
- **What the guidelines require of a submission:** a cover letter explaining why the manuscript suits the journal; manuscripts must fall "within the scope of the journal"; manuscripts not conforming to the guidelines "may be returned", and out-of-scope or unsuitable ones may be desk rejected without peer review (guidelines, retrieved 2026-11-23 via https://web.archive.org/web/20250615124017/https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/TAP ). Accepted types: articles, notes or comments (≤2,500 words), book reviews and review essays.
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** The consequence the journal states is return or desk rejection for non-conformity or out-of-scope work. Whether an empirical report without a theoretical claim would be judged out of scope is an inference from the scope sentence; no page read says so. Context count: "experiment/experimental" appears in 16 of 273 articles (5.9%), so empirical-experimental papers are published but rare (Crossref, 2026-11-23).

## Strategic Notes

Each line names the recorded fact it rests on.

### Hard Blockers

- Over 10,000 words counting abstract, footnotes and references: such papers are returned for trimming (Format > Word limit).
- AI-generated text or figures without disclosure and editor permission; non-disclosure may be treated as misconduct (Policies > AI Policy).
- Manuscripts that do not follow the guidelines (for example a manuscript that is not anonymised, or lacks the required declarations) "may be returned" (Format > Anonymisation; Framing Requirements).
- Posting an updated version of the manuscript to a preprint server during review is against the guidelines (Policies > Preprint Policy).

### Soft Tax

Revision burden: SciRev reports 2.0 review rounds and 3.4 months in total, from n=2 reviews, so treat it as indicative at best (Metrics > Review Cycle Time). Formatting burden: APA 7th, double-spacing, endnotes, a separate anonymised title page with ethics, funding, conflict and data declarations (Format). Scope risk, inferred: the stated scope is theoretical and meta-theoretical analysis, and only 16 of 273 recent articles (5.9%) mention experiments (Orientation), so an empirical manuscript should state its theoretical claim in the cover letter that the guidelines request. This last point is my inference; no page read states it.

### Best Suited For

- Conceptual or theoretical articles, which carry the journal's stated scope and 135 of 273 recent articles (Soft Metadata > Methodological Preferences).
- Critiques of measurement, quantification or the philosophy of psychological science: 5 of the 12 most-cited 2020–2025 works are on that theme (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Replies and commentary: recent issues include replies to earlier articles (for example 10.1177/09593543261493956, 10.1177/09593543261493962), and the guidelines set a 2,500-word limit for notes or comments (Format).
- Single-author work: 53% of recent journal articles have one author (Practical Concerns).
- Phenomenological or discourse-oriented work: "phenomenolog*" in 17 and "discourse/discursive" in 25 of 273 articles (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
