### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Stated as scope, not as a numbered submission rule. The aims page describes "an international forum for publishing research at the intersection of phenomenology, empirical science, and analytic philosophy of mind" and says the journal is "rooted in the phenomenological tradition", while allowing submissions to "engage with this tradition in variety of ways" (https://link.springer.com/journal/11097/aims-and-scope , read live 2026-11-23).
- **What the aims page asks for:** a dialogue between analytic and continental philosophy; discussion of phenomenological figures or topics in the context of philosophy of mind, perception, enactivism or social ontology is welcomed; research at the intersection of phenomenology and the cognitive sciences "broadly construed", from psychology and psychiatry to sociology, anthropology and education; empirical engagement "encouraged", but empirical studies are considered "typically" only if they bear clearly on a theoretical debate at that intersection (same URL).
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** the guide states only that submissions not meeting the guidelines may be rejected by the editors and that missing declarations make a submission "incomplete" (https://link.springer.com/journal/11097/submission-guidelines ). That a manuscript with neither phenomenological nor cognitive-science engagement would be refused is an inference from the scope text; no page read says so. Context count: phenomenology vocabulary appears in 186 of 513 records (36.3%) and the "empirical"/"participants" vocabulary in 13.9% of abstract-records.

## Strategic Notes

Each line names the recorded fact it rests on.

### Hard Blockers

- A manuscript that is not anonymized for double-blind review, or that lacks the required declarations: the guide says non-conforming submissions are returned as incomplete (Policies > Peer Review; Format > Other requirements).
- Listing an AI tool as an author: LLMs "do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria" (Policies > AI Policy).
- Undocumented LLM use beyond copy-editing: the guide asks that LLM use be documented in Methods; the stated consequence was not read (Policies > AI Policy).

### Soft Tax

Length: the guide gives about 9,000 words including references as the average and discourages more than 10,000 (Format > Word limit); a 10,000-word-plus manuscript has to be trimmed or justified. Framing: empirical studies are taken "typically" only if they bear on a theoretical debate at the phenomenology and cognitive-science intersection (Orientation), so an empirical report needs that argument stated up front; this reading is my inference from the aims text. Visibility: repository posting of the accepted manuscript waits 12 months on the subscription route, with immediate sharing on a personal website, and OA costs £2,390 / $3,290 / €2,690 (Policies > Preprint Policy, Open Access). Blind review: a public preprint is allowed by Springer's policy but the guide does not address double-blind interaction (Practical Concerns).

### Best Suited For

- Phenomenological analyses engaging the cognitive sciences: "phenomenolog*" in 186 of 513 records and enactive/embodied/4E vocabulary in 119 (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Phenomenologically grounded qualitative and interview methods papers: 6 of the 12 most-cited 2021-2026 works are of this kind (Epistemological & Political Leanings; Methodological Preferences).
- Phenomenological psychopathology and psychiatry: 44 of 513 records (8.6%) (Orientation).
- Purely theoretical papers in philosophy of mind or social ontology: the aims page says "articles may be purely theoretical" (Framing Requirements).
- Single-author work and papers near 9,000 words including references (Practical Concerns; Format).

### Not Recommended For

- Experimental or survey reports without a bearing on a phenomenological debate: aims sentence plus experiment vocabulary in 12 of 251 and survey vocabulary in 8 of 251 abstract-records (Framing Requirements; Methodological Preferences).
- Meta-analysis or mixed-method designs: 0 of 251 abstract-records each (Methodological Preferences).
- Manuscripts above 10,000 words including references that cannot be shortened (Format).
- Authors who need the accepted manuscript in a repository soon after acceptance and who will not use a personal website or the OA route (Policies > Preprint Policy).
