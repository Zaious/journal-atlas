### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- AD / dementia clinical research with strong methodology
- Biomarker discovery and validation studies
- Clinical trial reports (with CONSORT compliance)
- Multidisciplinary AD research (clinical + neuroimaging + biomarkers)

### Not Recommended For

- Pre-clinical animal-model only work (consider Neurobiology of Aging)
- Non-AD dementias without comparative AD framing
- Pure psychological-aging without AD/dementia specificity
