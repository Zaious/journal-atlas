### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes, as scope: the journal "publishes original articles of theoretical relevance for linking the notions of culture and psychology" and calls itself "the flagship journal on cultural psychology" (guidelines, mirror https://sage.cnpereading.com/storage/ext/submission-guidelines/page/author-instructions_CAP.html , retrieved 2026-11-23). Empirical evidence is expected "to be provided through qualitative methods"; in the 2024-12-08 copy, quantitative authors "are expected to prove in the manuscript that quantification of the phenomena under study is warranted" (https://web.archive.org/web/20241208065234/https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/CAP ).
- **What the guidelines require of a submission:** a cover letter saying why the manuscript suits the journal; a manuscript "within the scope of the journal" of "sufficient quality", else "it will not be reviewed" (the guidelines say so in the same preparation section); selection of one of five submission scopes (mirror); declarations on a separate title page. Aims statement: empirical work must be "relevant for theory" and "adequacy of the data to the phenomena is carefully scrutinized" (description page, https://web.archive.org/web/20220910193301/https://journals.sagepub.com/description/cap ).
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** The stated consequence is desk rejection without peer review for out-of-scope or unsuitable manuscripts and return of non-conforming ones (guidelines, peer review section). That a manuscript with culture only as a decorative variable would be judged out of scope is an inference from the scope sentences, not a rule on any page read. Count context: "experiment" appears in 5 of 299 abstracts (1.7%).
