### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes
- **If yes, what kind?** Cultural framing is mandatory — manuscripts must position phenomena within cultural-psychological theory. Universal-psychological claims without cultural contextualization are misaligned.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: Desk reject likely if culture is decorative rather than constitutive of the analysis; reviewer demand for "cultural specificity" if the framing is generic.

## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Manuscripts without explicit cultural framing or cultural-psychological theory engagement
- Submissions substantially exceeding 12,000 words inclusive of references (advisory but enforced)
- AI listed as author (per Sage publisher policy)
- Universal-psychological claims that ignore cultural variation

### Soft Tax

Moderate when manuscripts originate outside cultural-psychology paradigm. Authors typically invest ~1–2 revision sessions to: (1) recast generic-psychological framing into cultural-psychological terms, (2) engage Hermans / Valsiner / Bakhtin literature explicitly, (3) trim total length (including refs) to fit ~12,000-word advisory ceiling. Reference list typically consumes 1,800–2,200 of the 12K budget — main text effectively limited to ~10K.

### Best Suited For

- Dialogical self, cultural-narrative, and semiotic-cultural papers
- Cross-cultural comparative work with theoretical contribution
- Migration, diaspora, indigenous psychology, decolonial framings
- Identity and self studies foregrounding cultural constitution
- Autoethnographic work with strong cultural-positionality framing

### Not Recommended For

- Universal-psychological claims without cultural framing
- Quantitative experimental work without cultural variable
- BDSM / kink papers (zero published precedent 2015–2025)
- Papers requiring extensive reference lists beyond ~12K inclusive budget
- Pure cognitive-experimental work

### Rejection Fallback Chain

| Rank | Journal | Why this fallback works |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | [Theory & Psychology](./theory-and-psychology.md) | Broader theoretical scope (not exclusively cultural); larger main-text budget when references excluded; welcomes dialogical-self traditions |
| 2 | [Self and Identity](./self-and-identity.md) | Self / identity scope overlaps; smaller word limit but established outlet for identity-process work |
| 3 | [Qualitative Inquiry](../qualitative-methods/qualitative-inquiry.md) | Methodological fit for ethnographic and reflexive work; less doctrinal cultural-theoretical requirement |

**Reverse direction** *(optional)*: If your paper was rejected from a more general cross-cultural psychology outlet (e.g., *Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology*), Culture & Psychology is often a better fit because: it welcomes qualitative and interpretive methods rejected by quantitative cross-cultural journals; it embraces dialogical / narrative theoretical traditions; and it values cultural constitution of psychological phenomena rather than cross-national comparison alone.
