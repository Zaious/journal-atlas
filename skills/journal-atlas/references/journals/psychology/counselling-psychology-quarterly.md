Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Counseling psychology research with both quantitative and qualitative methods welcome
- Cross-cultural counseling contributions
- Counselor education / training research
- Authors comfortable with counseling-applied (not pure-academic) framing

### Not Recommended For

- Pure clinical psychology without counseling-applied angle
- Pure intervention efficacy trials (consider clinical psych journals)
- Counseling-adjacent organizational / I-O work
