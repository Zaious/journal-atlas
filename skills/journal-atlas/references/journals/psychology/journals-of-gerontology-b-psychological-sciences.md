## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Psychological aging research with strong methodology
- Longitudinal aging studies (Health and Retirement Study, etc.)
- GSA-community submissions
- Cross-cultural aging research

### Not Recommended For

- Pure cognitive aging without lifespan / wellbeing framing (consider J Gerontol A — Biological)
- Clinical-only aging without psychological contribution
- Younger-population studies without aging-comparison focus
