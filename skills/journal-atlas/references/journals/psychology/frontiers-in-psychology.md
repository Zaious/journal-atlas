### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Stated as scope and as article type, not as a theoretical stance. The about page says out-of-scope work includes pedagogy, linguistics, business, economics, management or AI "without a fundamental psychological basis"; purely technological work is excluded; simple bibliometric studies are no longer accepted; systematic reviews must follow PRISMA (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/about , read 2026-11-23).
- **What the guidelines require of a submission:** a 200-word scope statement (unpublished), an abstract and 5 to 8 keywords, an ethics statement for human or animal studies, and a data availability statement on every article (author guidelines https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/author-guidelines ; policies page https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/policies-and-publication-ethics , read 2026-11-23). The article type chosen sets the word limit (article-types page).
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** The policies page says incomplete data availability statements prevent a submission from entering review, and suspicious manuscripts "may be rejected before peer review" without explanation. The earlier statement that misaligned article types are moved or returned was not found on a page read, so it is withdrawn.

### AI-Research Notes (supplementary AI research, 2026-07-13)

**AI-research finding (policy):** Generative AI/LLMs permitted for writing and editing on condition of mandatory disclosure in the Acknowledgments (tool name/version/source); AI may NOT be listed as an author (cannot meet accountability criteria); authors remain fully responsible for factual accuracy of AI-generated text, citations, and figures. ## Strategic Notes

Each line names the recorded fact it rests on.

### Hard Blockers

- A manuscript with no "fundamental psychological basis" (pedagogy, linguistics, business, economics, management or AI work), or a simple bibliometric study: stated out of scope (Format > Article Types).
- Missing or incomplete data availability statement: "prevent[s] submission from entering review" (Soft Metadata > Practical Concerns).
- Human-participant research without institutional ethics approval and consent (Practical Concerns > IRB).
- Undisclosed AI-generated text or figures (Policies > AI Policy).
- A non-payable APC: A-type articles cost CHF 3,150 unless covered by an institution, reduced for low or lower-middle income countries, or supported by fee support (Policies > Open Access).
- Over the word limit of the chosen article type, e.g. 12,000 for Original Research, 2,000 for Opinion (Format > Word limit).

### Soft Tax

Cost: CHF 3,150 for A-type articles, CHF 2,500 for B-type (Open Access). Disclosure and data burden: a data availability statement on every article, FAIR-aligned minimal dataset, ethics statement, AI disclosure in acknowledgments and methods (Practical Concerns; AI Policy). Review: 2.3 rounds and 3.1 months total from n=33 SciRev reviews (Review Cycle Time). Scope: section-level fit could not be checked, since the section page showed no scope text (Subject Density); the journal-level exclusions apply (Format > Article Types).

### Best Suited For

- Quantitative survey and mediation-style empirical papers: "survey/questionnaire" in 34.6% and "structural equation / mediation" in 30.7% of 1,090 sampled abstracts (Methodological Preferences; Orientation).
- Authors who value a stated fast route: SciRev reports 3.1 months total handling (n=33) (Review Cycle Time).
- Work that fits one of the listed short article types with fixed limits: Brief Research Report 4,000, Perspective 3,000, Conceptual Analysis 8,000 (Format > Word limit).
- Systematic reviews that follow PRISMA and reach a psychology conclusion (Format > Article Types).
- Papers with a funder or institution that covers the APC (Open Access).

### Not Recommended For

- Authors who cannot pay an APC and have no institution or country route (Open Access).
- Pure bibliometric or purely technical (digital technology, neural network analysis) studies (about page scope, Format > Article Types).
- Autoethnographic or first-person reflective work, if precedent matters: 0 of 1,090 sampled abstracts, with the caveat that a sample can miss rare terms (Methodological Preferences).
- Work that cannot share its minimal dataset and cannot qualify for a stated exception (Practical Concerns > Data transparency).
- Single-author projects, if precedent matters: 8.2% of sampled records (Practical Concerns).
