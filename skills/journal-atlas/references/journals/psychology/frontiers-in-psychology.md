### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes (article-type specific)
- **If yes, what kind?** Manuscripts must fit one of the Frontiers article types. **Hypothesis & Theory** is the entry point for conceptual / theoretical work; **Original Research** requires empirical study. Manuscripts misaligned with article type are returned for restructuring.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: Manuscripts may be moved between article types during editorial assessment, or returned to authors. Failure to articulate the article-type fit can delay or block desk processing.

## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Inability to fit any Frontiers article type
- Failure to pay APC after acceptance (no general waiver fund — must qualify via institution or country)
- Submissions misaligned with the PSP section scope (consider other Frontiers sections)
- Failure to disclose AI use per Frontiers policy

### Soft Tax

Variable. For empirical PSP-aligned papers: low. For conceptual / theoretical papers: moderate — must explicitly frame as Hypothesis & Theory and provide rigorous literature synthesis. For qualitative / reflexive work: moderate — target the "Reflexive and Context-Sensitive" Research Topic or articulate methodological justification. **APC currency risk** ($4,032 USD baseline at 2026-05 exchange rate, invoiced in CHF) is a real cost factor; institutional Read & Publish agreements should be checked first.

### Best Suited For

- Empirical multi-study personality and social-psychology papers (Original Research A-Type)
- Hypothesis & Theory papers proposing new frameworks in PSP scope
- Systematic Reviews / Meta-Analyses on PSP topics
- Reflexive / qualitative work aligned with specific Research Topics
- Authors prioritizing rapid OA visibility (3–6 month total cycle)

### Not Recommended For

- Authors needing low-APC or zero-APC options (consider Collabra with SIPS discount, or subscription journals)
- Pure autoethnography or first-person work without reflexive-methodology framing
- Authors prioritizing reputational signaling in North American academic circles with Frontiers reservations
- Sensitive research where OA visibility conflicts with subject protection
- Manuscripts requiring extensive editorial development (Frontiers production cycle is fast)

### Rejection Fallback Chain

| Rank | Journal | Why this fallback works |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | [Collabra: Psychology](./collabra-psychology.md) | OA alternative with lower APC; SIPS open-science framing fits methodologically-rigorous PSP work |
| 2 | [Self and Identity](./self-and-identity.md) | If the paper is identity-focused quantitative social-psychology; T&F subscription model offers $0 submission |
| 3 | [Review of General Psychology](./review-of-general-psychology.md) | If the Hypothesis & Theory paper is integrative-theoretical; APA Division 1 reputation |

**Reverse direction** *(optional)*: If your paper was rejected from a more selective social-psychology outlet (e.g., *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*), Frontiers in Psychology PSP is often a better fit because: it offers a broader methodological and topical scope; reflexive Research Topics open space for non-mainstream methods; and the OA visibility and 3–6 month review cycle accelerate publication.
