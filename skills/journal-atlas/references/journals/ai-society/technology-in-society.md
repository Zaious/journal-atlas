## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Double anonymized review: a separate title page and an anonymised manuscript including references, figures and tables (Peer Review; Voice & Style).
- Scope is technology and its economic, social, political and cultural dynamics; the current aims emphasise comparative international research and impacts on management and innovation processes (Subject Density > Current positioning).
- Generative-AI use in writing is limited to readability and language and must be disclosed (AI Policy).
- A declaration of interest and CRediT author roles are required (Format; Practical Concerns).

### Soft Tax

- Cost: open access carries a USD 4,210 APC excluding taxes; the subscription route has no author fee (Open Access).
- Time: the journal reports 79 days to a decision after review and 195 days to acceptance; SciRev n=3 reports 4.8 months for the first round (Review Cycle Time).
- No word limit is stated in the guide read, so length norms must be taken from published articles, which this entry did not measure (Format > Word limit).

### Best Suited For

- Quantitative, empirical studies of technology adoption, innovation, ESG, digital transformation and AI in organisations or economies, particularly with Chinese or other comparative settings: these dominate titles and the most-cited records (Epistemological & Political Leanings; Methodological Preferences).
- Work framed for managers and policymakers, in line with the current aims text (Subject Density > Current positioning).
- Foresight and technology-assessment work, a stated aims topic with 22 of 1867 titles (Methodological Preferences).
- Authors wanting a fast first decision: the journal reports 9 days (Review Cycle Time).

### Not Recommended For

- Qualitative, ethnographic or critical-theoretical work as the main contribution: the current aims text names quantitative, mixed-methods and theoretical approaches only, and qualitative or interview wording appears in 12 of 1867 titles (Methodological Preferences).
- Authors who need the AI policy to allow more than language editing (AI Policy).
- Authors without access to an institutional open-access agreement who need open access (Open Access).
