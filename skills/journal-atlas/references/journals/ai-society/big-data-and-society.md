## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Fully anonymised manuscript plus a separate title page is mandatory [Peer Review > Type; Practical Concerns].
- Original research must fit 10,000 words including references; commentaries 3,000 [Format > Word limit].
- Papers that do not engage with the societal side of data and computation fall outside the stated scope (implications of Big Data for societies) [Subject Density > Current positioning].
- Ethics-committee approval details, or an explicit statement that approval was not required, are required for human-participant work [Practical Concerns].

### Soft Tax

- An APC is payable on acceptance and the current amount could not be verified (1,750 to 2,100 USD across archived pages); waivers must be requested [Open Access].
- No review-time information exists to plan around: the journal publishes none and SciRev has 0 reviews [Review Cycle Time].
- Disclosure of generative-AI content is required, naming model and purpose, while language-only help is free of disclosure [AI Policy].
- Competing against a topic mix with large shares in governance or accountability wording (147 of 695 abstracts, 21.2%) and sociotechnical framing (178, 25.6%) [Epistemological & Political Leanings].

### Best Suited For

- Critical, sociotechnical or governance-oriented studies of data, platforms and AI, since those vocabularies appear in 25.6% and 21.2% of abstracts and are the stated aim (representation, ontology and politics) [Epistemological & Political Leanings; Subject Density].
- Work using computational or innovative methods with reflection on societal consequences, where the description page states interest and 11.7% of abstracts (81 of 695) use such vocabulary [Methodological Preferences].
- Qualitative studies (interviews, ethnography, case studies), each appearing in 9 to 14% of abstracts [Methodological Preferences].
- Short, timely commentaries of up to 3,000 words, which the journal lists as a separate type and does not charge an APC for [Format; Open Access].

### Not Recommended For

- Purely technical contributions with no societal analysis, because the journal's key purpose is connecting debates about Big Data practices and their social consequences [Subject Density > Current positioning].
- Authors who cannot pay an APC and cannot obtain a waiver, other than via a commentary [Open Access].
- Work needing a documented, short review turnaround: no figure exists to rely on [Review Cycle Time].
