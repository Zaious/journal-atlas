## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Scope is STS: the description defines the journal as a forum for research on science and technology in relation to politics, economy, society and culture, and states out-of-scope manuscripts may be desk rejected (Framing Requirements; Peer Review).
- Anonymisation is required: a separate title page and a fully anonymised manuscript, with no author names in file names, headers or footers (Format; Voice & Style).
- Ethics approval or an explicit statement that none was required, plus a data availability statement, must be on the title page (Practical Concerns).
- Original articles are capped at 8,000 words excluding abstract, endnotes and references (Format > Word limit).

### Soft Tax

- Chicago 16th-edition author-date style and endnotes, which differs from APA-style journals (Format > Reference limit).
- Time: the guidelines give about one month for the editorial read and up to 6 months of review; SciRev n=2 reports 5.3 months total for accepted manuscripts (Review Cycle Time).
- No measured cycle-time data exists in Crossref, so planning rests on the journal's own statement and two reports (Review Cycle Time).
- Open access is a paid option at a Sage-set fee in the 3,000 to 4,000 USD range for most Sage titles; the journal-specific figure was not found (Open Access).

### Best Suited For
