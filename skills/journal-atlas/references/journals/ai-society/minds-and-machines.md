## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Double-blind anonymisation of manuscript, files and self-citations, with a separate title page, is required (Peer Review > Type; Format > Figure/Table limits).
- A "Statements and Declarations" section is required; submissions without it are returned as incomplete (Format > Figure/Table limits).
- Critiques of articles from other journals are generally not published (Format > Article types accepted).
- Editable source files (.docx or LaTeX) are required at each submission and revision (Format > Supplementary material).

### Soft Tax

- Time: publisher dates for accepted papers show a median of 221 days from received to accepted and 22 more days to first online publication (Review Cycle Time); the journal's own first-decision median is 45 days.
- Documentation of any LLM use in the Methods (AI Policy), plus the Springer Nature disclosure rules for AI-assisted drafting.
- Suggesting up to six independent reviewers in the submission system (Peer Review > Reviewer recommendations).
- Open access, if wanted, depends on an APC not confirmed here (Open Access).

### Best Suited For

- Philosophical conceptual analysis of AI, digital technology, cognition or computing, which the aims page names and which 41.3% of abstract-bearing records signal by wording (Subject Density > Orientation; Methodological Preferences).
- Work on epistemology, philosophy of science and logic applied to computer or data science (Epistemological & Political Leanings: 25 of 155 records, 16.1%).
- Ethics and governance of AI contributions (named editorial focus; top cited records are on AI ethics and auditing).
- Authors without an institutional affiliation, since the guidelines describe how unaffiliated authors are recorded (Practical Concerns).

### Not Recommended For

- Papers without a philosophical or conceptual argument, such as purely technical or purely empirical reports (the aims describe a philosophy journal seeking conceptual analyses; Subject Density > Current positioning).
- Critical-theory, decolonial or care-ethics framings as the main lens: such wording appears in 0 of 155 abstracts (Epistemological & Political Leanings), so the window shows no track record, though this is not evidence of rejection.
- Short opinion pieces beyond the commentary format (commentaries are capped at 4000; Format).
