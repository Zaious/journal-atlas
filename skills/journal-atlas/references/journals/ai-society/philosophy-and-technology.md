## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Double-anonymised review: manuscript and files must not identify the author (Peer Review > Type).
- A "Statements and Declarations" section is required; submissions without it are returned as incomplete (Format > Figure/Table limits).
- Commentaries are by invitation only, and reply articles, review-article proposals, symposia and special issues require contacting the journal first (Format > Article types accepted; Peer Review > Reviewer recommendations).
- Editable source files (.docx or LaTeX) are required at each submission and revision (Format > Supplementary material).

### Soft Tax

- Time: the journal states an average peer review time of 3 months; publisher dates for accepted papers show a median of 112 days from received to accepted (interquartile range 9 to 191, partly reflecting invited items) and 15 more days to first online publication (Review Cycle Time).
- Documentation of LLM use in the Methods other than copy editing (AI Policy).
- Length: research and review articles usually up to 10,000 words (Format > Word limit).
- Competition: output has risen from 54 records in 2021 to 224 in 2026 to date (Publication Frequency), and 13.0% of abstract-bearing records concern generative AI (Epistemological & Political Leanings).

### Best Suited For

- Philosophy-of-technology articles of any tradition, since the aims welcome "any tradition, school of thought or disciplinary background" and no theory vocabulary dominates (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Long-form arguments up to about 10,000 words (Format > Word limit).
- Work on AI and politics, healthcare, responsibility or moral status, which have open calls or the highest keyword shares (Sensitive Topics).
- Authors without an institutional affiliation: the guidelines describe how unaffiliated authors are recorded (Practical Concerns).

### Not Recommended For

- Short opinion or reply pieces that are not invited (Hard Blockers).
- Papers with no philosophical or conceptual argument, such as purely technical reports (aims: "conceptual nature and practical consequences"; Subject Density > Current positioning).
- Work needing a verified low-cost open access route: the APC was not confirmed here (Open Access).
