- Guidelines (archive 2025-06-12): unstructured abstract of 150 words, at least 4 keywords, a concise descriptive title, Sage Harvard references, Word files, a clear hierarchy of headings, quotations over 40 words displayed. https://web.archive.org/web/20250612103223/https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/NMS
- Measured abstract length over the 1,431 abstracts: median 147 words (range 24 to 345), so most sit near the stated 150.
- First person: 965 of 1,431 deposited abstracts (67.4%) contain we, our, I or my (Crossref, 2021-2026; a heuristic that also catches generic uses of "our"); 973 (68.0%) use "this article", "this paper", "this study" or "this essay" framing. - The guidelines carry no guidance on first person or tone.

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- The 8,000-word target counts everything including notes, references and tables, and manuscripts more than a few hundred words over it "will not be considered" [Format > Word limit].
- Manuscripts outside the scope of media and information change in society, or of insufficient quality, are not sent for review [Framing Requirements].
- An anonymised manuscript with a separate title page is required [Practical Concerns; Peer Review > Type].
- Ethics-approval statements (or a statement that approval was not needed) and a data availability statement are required [Practical Concerns].

### Soft Tax

- Review duration per SciRev averages a median of 11.7 weeks for the first round and 13.4 weeks in total for six users, with a 26.9-week maximum and one withdrawal after 97 days (n=7, self-reported) [Review Cycle Time].
- The 150-word abstract limit is tighter than the 250-word maximum at Big Data & Society and the 150 to 250 band at AI and Ethics (their entries in this directory); measured median here is 147 words [Format; Voice & Style].
- Open access costs a fee whose amount was not found [Open Access].
- Generative-AI content has to be disclosed with model and purpose [AI Policy].

### Best Suited For

- Studies of social media, platforms, politics, gender and youth, the topic mix that leads the OpenAlex topic list (Social Media and Politics 1,517 assignments) and the Crossref counts (politics 25.1%, labour and creators 17.3%) [Subject Density; Sensitive Topics].
- Qualitative and mixed empirical work as well as surveys and experiments, each present in 8 to 20% of abstracts [Methodological Preferences].
- Theory-led or conceptual work, 32.4% of abstracts, since the journal states it draws on both theoretical and empirical research [Methodological Preferences].
- Review articles surveying a media theme, a listed type that also produced two of the six most-cited records of the window [Format; Epistemological & Political Leanings].

### Not Recommended For

- Papers needing a firm, quick timeline: no journal-stated median exists and SciRev shows 5 to 27 weeks [Review Cycle Time].
- Long-form manuscripts that cannot fit within about 8,000 words including references [Format].
- Papers on computing systems with no societal analysis, because scope is the social dynamics of media and information change [Subject Density > Current positioning].
