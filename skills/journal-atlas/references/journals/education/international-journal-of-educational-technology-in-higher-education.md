| **Word limit negotiability** | The journal states it does not impose a strict word count; no exception procedure is described. |

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Scope is higher education only (Subject Density > Current positioning), and only Research Articles and Review Articles are listed as article types (Format > Article types accepted); short commentary or opinion formats are not offered.
- A data availability statement and the four other "Declarations" sections must be present in every manuscript (Format > Figure/Table limits; Practical Concerns).
- Anonymous submission for double-blind review is required (Peer Review > Type), although the guideline pages read do not describe the procedure.

### Soft Tax

- Length guidance of 4,500 to 8,000 words, stated as not strict (Format > Word limit).
- Declarations, reporting-guideline conformity (PRISMA, CONSORT, STROBE) and LLM-use documentation in the Methods (Format; AI Policy).
- Time: publisher dates for accepted papers show the median received-to-accepted interval rising from 83 days (2023) to 164 days (2025) and 169 days (2026 to date) (Review Cycle Time); the journal's own median to acceptance is 156 days.
- Competition for a limited topic niche: 92 of 390 records (23.6%) concern generative AI (Epistemological & Political Leanings).

### Best Suited For

- Higher-education technology studies, empirical or review, with no author charge and a CC BY licence (Open Access; Subject Density).
- Generative-AI-in-higher-education work: the five most-cited records of the window and 23.6% of records are on this theme (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Systematic and scoping reviews, since Review Articles are one of two types and 43 of 390 records (11.0%) mention review or meta-analysis (Methodological Preferences).
- Survey-based and mixed-methods studies: survey or questionnaire wording appears in 22.8% and mixed methods in 8.5% of records (Methodological Preferences).

### Not Recommended For

- Studies set outside higher education (Hard Blockers).
- Opinion, short-report or commentary pieces (Hard Blockers).
- Work framed mainly through critical, postdigital or autoethnographic framing alone: such wording appears in 2 and 3 of 390 records respectively (Epistemological & Political Leanings; Methodological Preferences), so the counts show little track record for them in this window.
