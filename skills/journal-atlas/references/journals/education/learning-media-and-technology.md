## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Scope**: the journal's live aims text says it is not looking to publish context-free studies of individual technologies in individual institutional settings, how-to guides for classroom technology use, or articles about optimising the potential of technology in education (Framing Requirements, https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/cjem20/about-this-journal, read in a real browser on 2026-11-24). A submission of those kinds is outside the stated scope.
- **AI use**: undisclosed generative AI use breaches the journal's own rule (statement in Methods or Acknowledgments with tool, version, how and why, plus a declaration), and unrevised AI-generated text or AI-altered research images breach the publisher policy (AI Policy, https://www.tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?show=instructions&journalCode=cjem20, https://taylorandfrancis.com/our-policies/ai-policy/).
- Length: Research Articles are capped at 8,000 words including the abstract, tables, captions, footnotes and endnotes (Format; live instructions). No other hard blocker is supported by a fact read; the ethics wording is publisher-level only.

### Soft Tax

- **Critical framing**: the aims text asks for a critical approach primarily from the social sciences, humanities and arts (Framing Requirements), and titles with "critical" are 26 of 364 (Methodological Preferences). A purely descriptive or evaluative technology study would have to be reframed to fit.
- **Open access cost**: green deposit of the accepted manuscript is subject to the publisher's 18-month embargo for humanities and social sciences (Preprint Policy; not confirmed for this journal); gold open access carries an APC whose amount was not read (Open Access).
- **Selectivity and timing**: the publisher's figures are a 5% acceptance rate, 6 days average to first decision and 90 days average to first post-review decision (Metrics, Review Cycle Time); the page does not say how many first decisions are desk rejections.

### Best Suited For

- Critical studies of digital platforms, data and AI in education: "AI/generative" 46, "platform" 40, "data/datafication" 34 of 364 titles, and the four most-cited records of the window are on these themes (Orientation, Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Work drawing on social science, cultural, media or policy perspectives: named in the aims text (Framing Requirements) and "policy" appears in 11 of 364 titles (3.0%).
- Multi-author teams: only 100 of 361 records are single-author (Voice & Style).

### Not Recommended For

- Practical or evaluative edtech studies, classroom how-to pieces and speculation about future technology: excluded by the journal's own aims text (Hard Blockers).
- Authors who need a published data-sharing mandate or an APC figure up front: the data policy is encouragement only and the APC amount was not readable (Practical Concerns, Open Access).
- Primarily quantitative studies: "quantitative" appears in no title and "survey" in 1 of 364 (Methodological Preferences); title-only evidence, so this shows rarity in titles, not rejection.
