- **Mandatory framing?** Yes, a breadth framing: the aims statement says focus must be broad enough for the wider education community and that small-scale evaluations of specific software or courses are excluded unless broader relevance is explicitly drawn out (https://web.archive.org/web/20261019081726/https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-and-education).

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Manuscripts above 8,000 words (references and appendices excluded) fall outside the stated limit (Format > Word limit).
- Small-scale evaluations of a specific system, course or single institution are not published unless broader relevance is made explicit (Format > Article types accepted; Framing Requirements).
- Papers that do not meet the criteria in the author guidelines are returned without review (Framing Requirements).
