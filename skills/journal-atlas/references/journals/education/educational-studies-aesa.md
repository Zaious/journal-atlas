## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Anonymisation**: a blinded text must be prepared alongside the complete text (Peer Review, Voice & Style).
- **Ethics confirmation**: authors confirm at submission that the research meets ethical guidelines, with IRB approval where applicable (association page), and the publisher's instructions require a Methods statement of ethical approval and a consent statement for research on people (Practical Concerns).
- **Single submission and format**: the manuscript must not be under consideration elsewhere, and files must be .doc or .docx (Format, association page).
- **AI use**: undisclosed or unrevised AI-generated content is outside the publisher policy (AI Policy).
- No other hard blocker is supported by a fact I could read. The length figures are called "suggested" (Format).

### Soft Tax

- **Time**: the publisher's block gives 29 days to first decision and 111 days to a first post-review decision, and a 15% acceptance rate, with no year printed (Metrics, Review Cycle Time); no submission-to-acceptance figure exists, and the unattributed SciRev page suggests several months, which cannot be relied on.
- **Open access cost**: gold open access carries an APC whose amount was not found (Open Access); the accepted manuscript is subject to the publisher's typical 18-month embargo for repository posting of non-open-access articles (Preprint Policy; not confirmed for this journal).
- **Length and style**: the two sources differ (Format): publisher instructions give 8000 words including references for a Research Article, a 150-word abstract and 4 to 6 keywords; the association page suggests 6000-8000 words, a 100-300-word abstract and APA 6th edition. Check the publisher's page at submission.

### Best Suited For

- Critical foundations scholarship in education on race, Indigeneity, decolonial and abolitionist themes, gender and queer studies, which the aims name and which fill the counts (race 38%, "Black" 22%, "critical race" 12%, decolonial or Indigenous 12%, gender 13%, queer 7.4%; Sensitive Topics).
