### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes, a contribution framing: the guidelines say articles must "take us beyond what we already know" and that a familiar method in a familiar setting needs a novel aspect (https://web.archive.org/web/20241126212856/https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/14678535/forauthors.html).
- **Required components:** structured practitioner notes (three headings), keywords, and for empirical work statements on open data and ethics; submissions missing them are returned (https://web.archive.org/web/20241126212856/https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/14678535/forauthors.html).
- **Preferred study shapes:** multi-site and longitudinal studies, reports of initiatives that failed, studies confirming or contradicting earlier BJET papers (https://web.archive.org/web/20241126212856/https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/14678535/forauthors.html, archive 2024-11-26).
- **Accessible-reader framing:** write for readers whose first language is not English, clarify age groups instead of national grade labels, spell out acronyms (same page).
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: the guidelines list desk-rejection grounds: out of scope, not far enough beyond existing knowledge, plagiarism, too long, poor English; about half of submissions are rejected on these grounds (same page).

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- A paper previously rejected by BJET will not be reconsidered even after revision (Peer Review > Reviewer recommendations); a substantially different paper needs prior consultation.
- A manuscript under 4,000 words is desk-rejected, and the count includes tables, figures and footnotes (Format > Word limit).
- Submissions lacking practitioner notes, keywords, or (for empirical work) data and ethics statements are returned before review (Framing Requirements).
- Manuscripts must be anonymised for double-anonymous review (Peer Review > Type), which constrains how prior work and named systems can be described (Practical Concerns > OPSEC).

### Soft Tax

- Length discipline: normal range 5,000 to 6,000 words with tables and footnotes counted; longer needs a prior email and justification (Format).
- Practitioner notes and a contribution statement beyond existing knowledge add writing work (Framing Requirements; Format > Abstract limit).
- Time: for accepted papers the median received-to-accepted interval was 183 days and has risen from 147 days in 2021 to 215 in 2024 (Review Cycle Time); this excludes rejected papers.
- About half of submissions are desk-rejected (Metrics > Desk Rejection Rate), so scope and novelty framing carry weight before any review.

### Best Suited For

- Empirical studies of educational technology (experiments, surveys, interviews and learning-analytics designs together account for most keyword hits: Methodological Preferences) with a stated practical implication and a clear methodological description (Framing Requirements).
