### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Integrative review framing: the journal publishes "critical, integrative reviews" and "does not publish original empirical research, and all analyses should be incorporated in a broader integrative review" (older guidelines page, archive 2023-04-28: https://web.archive.org/web/20230428040342/https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/RER).
- **Educational relevance:** a review from any discipline must "bear on educational issues" (description, archive 2022-06-11: https://web.archive.org/web/20220611194146/https://journals.sagepub.com/description/RER).
- **Reporting standards:** authors consult the research-reporting standards adopted by the AERA Council (named "Standards for Reporting on Research in AERA Publications" on the 2023-04-28 page; the first words of that sentence are cut off in the 2024 print); systematic reviews and meta-analyses also consult PRISMA (guidelines print, archive 2025-04-23: https://web.archive.org/web/20250423115227id_/https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/submission-guidelines_-review-of-educational-research_-sage-journals.pdf?sfvrsn=6b27d2ba_3).
- **Scope check:** the guidelines tell authors to check the journal's Aims and Scope before submitting (older page, 2023-04-28); review-wording in titles or abstracts: 122 of 263 records mention a systematic review and 80 a meta-analysis (Crossref count, retrieved 2026-11-23).
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: submissions that do not meet the formatting requirements "will be returned" and over-length manuscripts "may be returned without review" (guidelines print); no stated consequence for failing the integrative-review framing beyond editors' judgment.

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above; the source row is named in brackets.

### Hard Blockers

- Original empirical research is not accepted outside a broader integrative review (Format > Article types accepted; Framing Requirements).
- Manuscripts over 65 pages including references "may be returned without review" (Format > Word limit).
- Word-only submission with a fully anonymised manuscript; non-conforming files are returned (Format > Figure/Table limits; Peer Review > Type).
- The published acceptance rate was 7.2% in 2025 and 5.4% in 2024 (Metrics > Acceptance Rate).

### Soft Tax

- References count toward the cap, so long reference lists from large systematic searches consume pages; analysed-but-uncited studies can move to a supplementary list (Format > Word limit negotiability).
- PRISMA consultation for systematic reviews and meta-analyses, and disclosure of generative-AI use (Framing Requirements; AI Policy > Summary).
- Unclear turnaround: no decision-time figure is published and the one SciRev entry is a 21-day immediate rejection, a single data point (Review Cycle Time).
- Abstract of 150 words or fewer, while the deposited median is 151 (Voice & Style).

### Best Suited For

- Quantitative research syntheses of education interventions: meta-analysis wording appears in 30.4% of records and systematic-review wording in 46.4% (Methodological Preferences).
- Conceptual or critical integrative reviews with a clear framework: framework or theoretical wording in 29.7% (Orientation; Methodological Preferences).
- Reviews on teacher, literacy and higher-education topics, which lead the window's subject counts (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Equity-focused reviews, including decolonial work: equity wording in 21.3% of records and a decolonizing-curriculum review among the five most-cited records (Sensitive Topics).

### Not Recommended For

- Primary empirical studies of any design, qualitative or quantitative (Hard Blockers).
- Reviews that cannot fit 65 double-spaced pages with references (Hard Blockers).
- Narrow descriptive literature summaries without a conceptual argument: the journal asks for "critical, integrative" reviews with "conceptualizations, interpretations, and syntheses" (Framing Requirements).
- Authors who need a published turnaround guarantee (Review Cycle Time).
