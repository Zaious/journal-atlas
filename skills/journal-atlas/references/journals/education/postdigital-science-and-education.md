## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Scope**: the aims text frames the journal around the postdigital age, and the guidelines judge articles partly on "relevance to Postdigital Science and Education" (Framing Requirements; https://link.springer.com/journal/42438/submission-guidelines). A paper with no engagement with postdigital themes risks being out of scope; the journal does not state a consequence.
- **Anonymisation**: review is double-blind and the author must anonymise the manuscript and files (Peer Review, Practical Concerns); an identifiable manuscript does not meet the stated procedure.
- **Ethics approval**: retrospective ethics approval usually cannot be obtained and the Editor may decline to send such a paper to review (Practical Concerns).
- **AI use**: AI cannot be listed as an author, and LLM use must be documented in Methods (AI Policy).

### Soft Tax

- **Open access cost**: gold open access costs GBP 2390 / USD 3290 / EUR 2690 plus tax, due after acceptance (Open Access); the green route carries a 12-month embargo for subscription-route accepted manuscripts (Preprint Policy, publisher table).
- **Length**: Research Articles are expected at about 7,000 words, and whether references count is not stated (Format); departures are negotiated with the Editor-in-Chief.

### Best Suited For

- Theory-led and conceptual work on digital technology, AI and education: "theory/theoretical" 50 and "conceptual" 32 of 164 abstracts, and "artificial intelligence/AI/generative" 44 of 164 (Orientation, Sensitive Topics).
- Dialogic and collective formats: Q&A/Debate Articles and open "Postdigital Dialogues" and collective-article calls (Format, Framing Requirements).
- Authors who want a quick first decision: the publisher reports a median of 8 days (Review Cycle Time).
- Single authors and small teams: 277 of 518 records are single-author (Voice & Style).

### Not Recommended For

- Work with no postdigital framing: 117 of 164 abstracts and 202 of 518 titles use the term (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Authors who cannot pay an APC and need open access without an institutional agreement: the APC is GBP 2390 or more (Open Access).
- Primarily quantitative or mixed-methods studies: "quantitative" appears in 1 and "mixed method" in none of 164 abstracts (Methodological Preferences); this shows rarity, not rejection.
