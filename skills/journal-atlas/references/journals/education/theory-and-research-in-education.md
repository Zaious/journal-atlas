## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Scope**: out-of-scope or unsuitable manuscripts may be desk rejected without review (Framing Requirements, Peer Review); the stated scope is theoretical, empirical and conjectural work on educational theory, policy and practice.
- **Anonymisation**: the manuscript must be fully anonymised with a separate title page (Peer Review, Practical Concerns).
- **AI use**: undisclosed generative-AI use can lead to rejection at any stage (AI Policy).
- **Statements**: ethics approval or waiver, consent, conflict and funding statements are required on the title page (Practical Concerns).
- No other hard blocker is supported by a fact read; the live guidelines state no word limit (Format).

### Soft Tax

- **Time**: the journal's metrics page prints medians of 34 days to first decision, 148 days for 'peer review' and 254 days from submission to acceptance, with no year and no definition of the 148-day interval (Review Cycle Time); the journal says two independent reviews are needed before a revise or accept decision (Peer Review), and the page gives a 5.0% acceptance rate (Metrics).
- **Open access cost**: open access is for a fee whose amount was not read (Open Access); accepted manuscripts can be shared by the author after acceptance (Preprint Policy).
- **Preprint ambiguity**: the journal's own guidelines contradict each other on preprints (Preprint Policy), so authors would need to ask the editor first.

### Best Suited For

- Normative and analytic philosophy of education on justice, democracy, autonomy, virtue or moral education: "moral" 25%, "justice" 24%, "democra" 21%, "autonomy" 12%, "virtue" 12% of abstracts-plus-titles (Methodological Preferences, Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Sole-authored essays: 78% of records with authors are single-author (Voice & Style).
- Work that links philosophy to political science, law, sociology or history, which the guidelines name as welcome (Orientation).

### Not Recommended For

- Primarily empirical or quantitative studies: "quantitative", "survey", "questionnaire" and "experiment" appear in 0 of 118 abstracts (Methodological Preferences). The counts show these are absent from the sample, not that they would be rejected, and the aims accept empirical papers.
- Continental and phenomenological work: "phenomenolog", Foucault, Habermas and Kant appear in 0 of 118 (Epistemological & Political Leanings); same caveat.
- Anyone who needs a known word limit or APC before choosing: neither is stated on the live pages (Format, Open Access).
