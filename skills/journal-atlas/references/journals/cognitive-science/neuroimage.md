### Best Suited For

- Neuroimaging method development
- Large-scale neuroimaging studies (Human Connectome Project, UK Biobank)
- Connectomics and network neuroscience
- Computational neuroimaging contributions

### Not Recommended For

- Non-imaging neuroscience (consider J Neurosci / Cereb Cortex)
- Single-subject case studies without methodological contribution
- Pure behavioral cognitive psychology without imaging
