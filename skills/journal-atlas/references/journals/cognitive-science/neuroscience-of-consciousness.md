## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- *(community estimate; submission experience welcome)*

### Soft Tax

Variable; depends on closeness of fit between manuscript framing and the journal's scope.

### Best Suited For

- Consciousness research with rigorous neuroscience grounding
- Theoretical models of consciousness (IIT / GWT / HOT)
- Empirical studies of NCCs (neural correlates of consciousness)
- Authors committed to ASSC community framing

### Not Recommended For

- Pure philosophy of mind without empirical or computational content
- General cognitive neuroscience without consciousness specificity
- Speculative work without theoretical or methodological rigor
