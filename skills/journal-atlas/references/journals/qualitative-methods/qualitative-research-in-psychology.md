### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Yes (soft)
- **If yes, what kind?** Manuscripts must engage **qualitative methodology in psychology** — either through methodological innovation, conceptual contribution, or exemplary methodological execution. Theoretical papers must contribute to debates about qualitative research; empirical papers must illustrate methodology in an exemplary or innovative way.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: Desk reject likely for purely descriptive empirical reports without methodological reflection; reviewer pushback on insufficient methodological exemplification.

## Strategic Notes

### Hard Blockers

- Quantitative-only submissions
- Empirical reports without methodological reflection or exemplification
- Manuscripts outside psychology scope (consider Qualitative Inquiry or Qualitative Research instead)
- AI listed as author (per T&F publisher policy)

### Soft Tax

Low for qualitative-psychology authors. The journal's "exemplary qualitative methodology" expectation may require minor restructuring to foreground the methodological contribution. For ethnographic / autoethnographic work originating outside psychology, soft tax is moderate — must engage qualitative-psychology canon (Braun & Clarke, IPA, etc.). No hard word-limit pressure (median 22 pages observed; up to 39 pages with content justification).

### Best Suited For

- Qualitative-methodology innovation papers in any psychology subfield
- IPA, thematic analysis, discursive psychology, narrative psychology empirical work
- Theoretical debates about qualitative research in psychology
- Disclosure, identity-process, mental-health, and self-related qualitative studies
- Reflexive autoethnographic work with qualitative-psychology framing
- BDSM / kink / sensitive-topic qualitative-psychology research (precedent exists)

### Not Recommended For

- Quantitative or quantitative-dominant mixed-methods submissions
- Submissions framed primarily for non-psychology audiences (consider Qualitative Inquiry)
- Manuscripts requiring rapid OA visibility (12-month T&F AAM embargo estimate)
- Pure performance ethnography or arts-based inquiry (consider Qualitative Inquiry or Cultural Studies ↔ Critical Methodologies)

### Rejection Fallback Chain

| Rank | Journal | Why this fallback works |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | [Qualitative Inquiry](./qualitative-inquiry.md) | Closest methodological cousin; more accommodating of experimental and performance forms; broader interdisciplinary scope |
| 2 | [Cultural Studies ↔ Critical Methodologies](./cultural-studies-critical-methodologies.md) | If the work has critical / decolonial / performance grounding; SAGE qualitative family |
| 3 | [Theory & Psychology](../psychology/theory-and-psychology.md) | If the theoretical contribution is foreground over methodological exemplification |

**Reverse direction** *(optional)*: If your paper was rejected from a top mainstream psychology outlet (e.g., *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, *Psychological Methods*) for being "too qualitative," Qualitative Research in Psychology is often a better fit because: it is the qualitative-psychology family's flagship for exemplary methodology; it accepts disclosure-related and identity-process work that mainstream outlets reject as "not generalizable"; and word-limit flexibility (up to ~15K) accommodates longer methodological argumentation.
