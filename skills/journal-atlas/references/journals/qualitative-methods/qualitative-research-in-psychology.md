### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Stated as scope: the journal "seeks innovative and pioneering work that moves the field forward" and is a forum for "qualitative researchers in all areas of psychology" (home page, Wayback 2025-11-06: https://web.archive.org/web/20251106024838/https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/uqrp20 ). The aims add "a showcase for exemplary and innovative qualitative research projects in psychology" (about page, Wayback 2025-01-04 URL in Identity).
- **What the older author PDF requires of article types:** theoretical papers must address conceptual issues or contribute to debates on qualitative research; empirical papers should illustrate qualitative methodology "in an exemplary manner" or use a qualitative approach "in unusual or innovative ways" (https://files.taylorandfrancis.com/uqrpauth.pdf ). The 2023 instructions page does not repeat the type definitions.
- **What the instructions require of a submission:** the editor first assesses a paper for suitability and only then sends it to two referees (instructions snapshot 2023-06-27: https://web.archive.org/web/20230627085624/https://www.tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?show=instructions&journalCode=uqrp20 ). The about page adds "initial editor screening" (Wayback 2025-01-04).
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** the stated consequence is failure at the editor's suitability check. That a purely descriptive empirical report without methodological contribution would fail it is my reading of the "exemplary and innovative" wording; no page read says so. Context count: "quantitative/survey/questionnaire" in 3 of 192 titles (1.6%) (Crossref, 2026-11-23).
