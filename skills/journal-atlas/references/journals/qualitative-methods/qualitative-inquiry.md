### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Stated as scope: the journal "seeks submissions that attend to the development of qualitative methodologies and approaches to inquiry, rather than those manuscripts which simply use a qualitative method or technique in the conduct of a research project" (guidelines mirror, read 2026-11-23: https://sage.cnpereading.com/storage/ext/submission-guidelines/page/author-instructions_QIX.html ).
- **What the journal says it publishes:** research articles "that experiment with manuscript form and content, and focus on methodological issues raised by qualitative research rather than the content or results of the research" (home page, Wayback 2026-10-17: https://web.archive.org/web/20261017113422/https://journals.sagepub.com/home/qix ); the 2022 description page adds think-pieces, review essays, postmodern or post-structural treatments, practical applications and theoretical discussions of philosophical bases (Wayback 2022-01-21: https://web.archive.org/web/20220121121144/https://journals.sagepub.com/description/qix ).
- **What the guidelines require of a submission:** the article "must be within the scope of the journal and be of sufficient quality. If not, it will not be reviewed"; non-conforming manuscripts "may be returned" and out-of-scope ones may be desk rejected without peer review (mirror).
- **Consequences of ignoring it:** the stated consequence is return or desk rejection. That an empirical report without methodological contribution would be judged out of scope follows from the scope sentence above; no page read states that case separately. Context count: "theoriz*/theoretical/conceptual" in 96 of 633 abstracts (15.2%) and "quantitative/survey/questionnaire" in 10 (1.6%) (Crossref, 2026-11-23).

## Strategic Notes

Each line names the recorded fact it rests on.

### Hard Blockers

- Over 8,000 words inclusive of notes and references: stated limit (Format > Word limit).
- A manuscript that only uses a qualitative method without a methodological contribution falls outside the stated scope and risks return or desk rejection (Framing Requirements).
- AI-generated text, references or images used without disclosure, or an AI chatbot listed as an author (Policies > AI Policy).
- Posting an updated version of the manuscript to a preprint server during review is against the guidelines (Policies > Preprint Policy).
- A manuscript that is not fully anonymised, with names in the file name, headers or footers, does not meet the guidelines and "may be returned" (Format > Anonymisation).

### Soft Tax

Length: the 8,000-word ceiling includes notes and references, so references reduce the main text one for one (Format > Word limit); abstract is capped at 100-150 words (Format). Framing: the scope asks for methodological development rather than a method used, so a paper written as an empirical report would need recasting (Framing Requirements; Orientation: 96 of 633 abstracts name theory or concepts). Cycle time is unknown: no time to decision, rounds or acceptance rate could be sourced (Metrics > Review Cycle Time).

### Best Suited For

- Methodological and philosophy-of-inquiry papers: the stated scope and "theoriz*/theoretical/conceptual" in 96 of 633 abstracts (Methodological Preferences).
- Ethnography and autoethnography: 132 and 82 of 633 abstracts; both named in the scope text (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Arts-based, poetic and performance inquiry: "arts as research" named in the scope; poetry 68, performance 52, arts-based 38 of 633 abstracts (Methodological Preferences).
- Posthuman, new-materialist and post-qualitative work: 55 of 633 abstracts and named in the scope (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Decolonial and Indigenous methodologies: 50 of 633 abstracts and named in the scope (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Work on generative AI in qualitative research: 48 of 633 abstracts (7.6%) and items on the Sage most-read and most-cited lists (Policies > AI Policy).

### Not Recommended For

- Quantitative or mixed-methods work: "mixed method" 0 and "quantitative/survey/questionnaire" 10 of 633 abstracts (Methodological Preferences).
- Manuscripts that need more than 8,000 words including references (Format).
- Papers whose contribution is a substantive finding rather than a methodological one (Framing Requirements).
- Work relying on undisclosed generative-AI content, or on AI-simulated interview participants: listed as inappropriate by Sage's policy (Policies > AI Policy).
- Topics not observed in the window, if topic fit matters: BDSM/kink and sex work, 0 of 633 abstracts each; drug use 2, suicide or self-harm 3 (Sensitive Topics). This does not show these topics would be refused.
