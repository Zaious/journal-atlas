### Framing Requirements

- **Mandatory framing?** Scope rule: the journal welcomes "any previously unpublished article focused on games and gaming" but its primary focus is aesthetic, cultural and communicative aspects of computer games, and proposed articles "should be jargon-free" (about page copy 2026-01-02, https://web.archive.org/web/20260102201450id_/https://gamestudies.org/2501/about).
- **What kind of engagement?** Articles should "shed new light on games" rather than treat games as a metaphor or illustration of another theory (about page); the guidelines encourage "interdisciplinary approaches where the main focus is computer games" (guidelines copy 2025-12-04, https://web.archive.org/web/20251204211103id_/https://gamestudies.org/2501/submission_guidelines).
- **Article type and length**: articles up to 8000 words with a 300-word abstract; reviews by invitation only (see Format).
- **Special-issue framing seen**: the call "Game Analysis Perspectives (GAP)" in issue 21.3 (https://gamestudies.org/2103/articles/cfp_game_analysis_perspectives); the guidelines prefer a continuous flow of general submissions.
- **Consequences of ignoring it**: "Failure to follow these submission guidelines will result in your paper being returned to you" (guidelines copy 2025-12-04). The copy dated 2026-04-18 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260418203749id_/https://gamestudies.org/2601/submission_guidelines) says submissions are closed "due to an unusually high volume of submissions in 2025"; no reopening date is given.

## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Submissions closed at last record**: the guidelines copies of 2025-12-04 and 2026-04-18 say submissions are closed for the time being; the 2026-04-18 copy gives "an unusually high volume of submissions in 2025" as the reason and names no reopening date (https://web.archive.org/web/20260418203749id_/https://gamestudies.org/2601/submission_guidelines). The live status was not readable (Format, AI-Research Notes).
- **No prior online availability**: the checklist requires that the article has not been published or made available online, which conflicts with posting a public preprint (Preprint Policy).
- **Length and anonymity**: 8000 words, abstract at most 300 words, double-blind anonymisation including removal of self-references (Format, Peer Review).
- **One submission at a time**: only one submission per author or team is reviewed at once (Practical Concerns).

### Soft Tax

- **Selectivity**: the 2015 editorial reports an 85-90% rejection rate; that figure is old (Metrics).
- **Submission by email with a house style**: a modified APA style, preferred-spelling list, image-size limits and a short description of 350 characters add editing work (Format).
- **No timing information**: no stated review-time target and no SciRev page exist (Review Cycle Time).

### Best Suited For

- Humanities-leaning analysis of games: aesthetics, culture, ontology and hermeneutics, as in the window titles listed under Dominant theoretical tradition (Epistemological & Political Leanings).
- Authors who want a non-profit open-access venue with no fee statement and can accept that submissions are closed with no reopening date given (Open Access, Hard Blockers).
- Game analysis and close reading, given the Game Analysis Perspectives call (Methodological Preferences).

### Not Recommended For

- Authors with a public preprint, because the checklist requires that the article is not available online (Preprint Policy).
- Quantitative, experimental or education-focused studies: 1 of 98 titles carries quantitative wording and 1 carries learning wording, so the evidence base here is thin (Methodological Preferences).
- Authors who need an AI policy, ethics rule, timing or fee statement before submitting: none could be read (AI Policy, Practical Concerns).
