## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Length**: 8000 words including tables, figures, abstract and references, with a 10% leeway; abstract at most 150 words; non-conforming papers "will be returned" (Format).
- **Anonymisation**: manuscripts must be fully anonymised with identifying information on a separate Title Page (Peer Review).
- **Ethics statement**: human-participant work needs a named committee, institution and approval number, or a stated waiver (Practical Concerns).
- **Scope**: out-of-scope papers are not reviewed (Framing Requirements).

### Soft Tax

- **Time**: a three-month initial-review target against SciRev's 4.2-month first-round mean with n = 3 (Review Cycle Time); the two are not reconciled.
- **Cost**: no submission or publication fee; optional open access carries a fee whose amount was not read (Open Access).
- **Reference budget**: references count inside the 8000 words, which cuts the room for a long literature section (Format).

### Best Suited For

- Qualitative, theoretical and textual or discourse-based studies of games and play culture: G3 and G6 each reach 19.7% of abstract-records and G9 9.7% (Methodological Preferences).
- Work on players, game design, gender, politics and harassment, the largest wording themes in the window (Top Topics, Sensitive Topics).
- Authors from outside game studies departments, since the instructions name ten fields (Framing Requirements).

### Not Recommended For

- Papers needing more than about 8800 words including references: the limit counts references (Format).
- Technical game-AI or engineering papers: AI or machine-learning wording is 6 of 319 abstract-records (1.9%, G10), so the evidence base here is thin (Orientation).
- Authors who need a fixed decision time or acceptance rate: neither is published (Review Cycle Time, Metrics).
