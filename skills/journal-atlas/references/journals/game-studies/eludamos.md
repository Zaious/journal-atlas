## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **Length**: articles up to 8,000 words excluding references, notes and tables; one submission per author at a time (Format).
- **Anonymisation**: the manuscript and its file metadata must be fully anonymised; non-compliant manuscripts are returned (Peer Review, Framing Requirements).
- **Scope exclusion**: articles on technical advances in game design or instrumental, marketing or gamification-effectiveness approaches are not published (Framing Requirements).
- **AI use**: chatbots as co-authors or sources are not accepted, and a title-page AI statement is required when AI tools were used (AI Policy).
- **Ethics**: informed consent and compliance with institutional and national research-ethics rules (Practical Concerns).

### Soft Tax

- **Time**: a three-month initial-review aim that the journal says can be exceeded during busy periods (Review Cycle Time).
- **Preprint ambiguity**: two pages disagree on when a preprint may be posted, so authors should ask the editors before posting (Preprint Policy).
- **Style guide**: a required house style guide (APA 7, Times New Roman 12 point, 1.5 spacing, footnotes, ORCID iD on the title page, no PDF or TeX) adds formatting work before review (Format).

### Best Suited For

- Critical, cultural and political analysis of games, play and the game industry, in line with the scope list and the 17.6% politics wording and 16.2% gender wording of abstract-records (Sensitive Topics).
- Authors who need a no-fee, CC BY open-access venue with no embargo (Open Access, Preprint Policy).
- Qualitative, theoretical, ethnographic and close-analysis designs: G3, G6, G4 and G9 each reach 7.4% or more of 68 abstract-records (Methodological Preferences).

### Not Recommended For

- Technical, instrumental or effectiveness-oriented work on game design, gamification or serious games: excluded by the scope statement, and learning wording is 5 of 68 abstract-records (7.4%, G17) (Framing Requirements).
- Authors who need a fixed decision time: the stated aim is three months with a caveat (Review Cycle Time).
- Quantitative or experimental studies: experiment wording is 0 of 68 (G2) and quantitative wording 4 (5.9%, G1) (Methodological Preferences).
