## Strategic Notes

> Each statement below follows from a fact recorded above and names it. Where the evidence does not support a statement, the field says so.

### Hard Blockers

- **No unsolicited submissions**: the journal "does not currently accept unsolicited submissions" and takes papers only in response to a specific call (Framing Requirements; https://todigra.org/index.php/todigra/information/authors, read 2026-11-23).
- **Prior and parallel publication**: the work must not be published or under consideration elsewhere (Practical Concerns).

### Soft Tax

- **Call dependence**: no open call was found on the pages read, so the timing of a submission depends on a call announced elsewhere (Framing Requirements).
- **No policy text**: peer-review model, word limit, abstract limit and preprint rule could not be read, so authors must ask the editors before writing (Format, Peer Review).
- **Low citation counts**: the most-cited window records have 4 or fewer Crossref citations (Epistemological & Political Leanings); this describes visibility, not quality.

### Best Suited For

- Authors who have a paper that fits an announced call, such as a thematic or regional special issue (the current one is the Chinese DiGRA Special Issue) (Framing Requirements).
- Qualitative, ethnographic and discourse-oriented game studies and design-oriented work: G3 and G9 each reach 9.5% of 42 abstract-records and G20 28.6% (Orientation).
- Possibly work in languages other than English: two Spanish-language titles appear among the eight most-cited window records, but with only 3 and 2 Crossref citations (checked 2026-11-24), which is too thin to infer how such work is received (Orientation).

### Not Recommended For
