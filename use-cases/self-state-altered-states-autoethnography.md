- **Methodology**: single-author autoethnography; theoretical paper grounded in self-state ecosystem literature

## Key takeaways

- **The OA + budget interaction is the most dramatic constraint shift.** "Immediate OA at $0 budget" doesn't just narrow candidates — it inverts the ranking. The previously-best journals collapse and unconventional survivors emerge.
- **Publisher-level embargo policy is decisive.** Sage's zero-embargo Green-OA window is the strategic asset that makes RGP, QI, and C&P winnable under tight constraints; Taylor & Francis (12–18 months), Springer (12), and Elsevier (24) all fail this test.
- **Rejection-fallback walking generates real strategy.** When PCS rejects for "insufficient phenomenological framing," the right move isn't to redo the framing — it's to switch journal tradition entirely. The skill's fallback chain encodes that escape route.
- **Survivor bias is a feature, not a bug.** RGP wasn't the methodological best fit — it was what survived after every hard constraint was applied. The skill explains this transparently when asked.
- **Honest "I don't know" beats hallucinated recommendation.** The biology question is the test that matters most for trust: a journal recommender that fakes coverage outside its knowledge base is worse than useless.

## Skill behavior notes

- **Discovery worked without explicit invocation.** The author just said "I have a psychology paper to submit" — the skill activated and entered the Step 1 attribute-extraction protocol on its own.
- **Surprised positively: Gold-vs-Green-OA reasoning was not explicit in SKILL.md.** The skill derived from publisher-level data in journal files (embargo lengths, Sage / Springer / T&F policies) and synthesized a strategic framework that goes beyond the recipe. This suggests SKILL.md + journal entries provide enough density for emergent strategic reasoning.
- **Surprised positively: the "Trojan-horse pivot" framing.** Describing the post-rejection RGP submission as a "Trojan horse" — disguising autoethnography as integrative theory — is a strategically useful metaphor that wasn't in the prompt or the SKILL.md. The skill produced it as an explanatory aid.
- **One technical hiccup**: in the comparison-mode response (Q6), the first rendering attempt was truncated by a loop-detection guard in the AI host environment, and the table had to be re-emitted. Not a skill defect; an environmental artifact. Worth tracking in case other authors hit it.
- **The meta-question response (Q7) is the most reassuring single output.** When asked to explain its own reasoning, the skill produced a coherent, honest account of the prioritization logic — including admitting that RGP only "won" because the constraint stack progressively eliminated its competitors.
